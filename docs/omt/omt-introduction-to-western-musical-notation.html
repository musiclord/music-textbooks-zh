西方記譜法入門

Chelsey Hamm

重點整理

  • 「西方記譜法」指的是用來傳達音樂聲響的書寫符號。
  • 不同時代、地點與文化都有各種記譜系統；西方記譜法只是其中之一。
  • 把音樂寫下來的人，會依這些因素，也就是其社會脈絡或環境（milieu），選擇記譜系統。

音樂是一種聽覺的，也就是 aural 的藝術形式，可以透過記譜將它轉為視覺呈現。

想一想，把一種語言翻成另一種語言是怎麼回事。這項工作可能很有挑戰性：逐字直譯不一定可行，也不一定有用，因此譯者必須思考如何把意思傳達給讀者。同樣地，把聲音寫下來，也必須決定要傳達什麼，以及如何傳達。沒有任何記譜系統能把聲音的每個面向都完整詳錄，同時還保持實用、可讀；因此，記譜只能呈現其中一部分音樂要素。

即使如此，書面記譜仍有許多好處。例如，把音樂作品寫下來，就不必完全依靠記憶，也能記錄原本可能遺忘的細節。此外，記譜能讓更多人接觸音樂作品：只要懂得那套記譜系統，就能自行學習已記下的作品，不必由教師協助。

試著想像，你要發明一種方法，把喜愛的歌曲或樂曲寫下來，讓音樂家即使從未聽過，也能演奏。在下方「發明一套記譜系統」的作業中，你將有機會選擇一首歌曲或樂曲來實際嘗試。聆聽並思考如何建立自己的記譜系統時，可以考慮以下問題：

  1. 作品中哪些音樂特徵比較重要？哪些比較不重要？
  2. 你想記下哪些音樂特徵？有沒有打算省略的部分？
  3. 如果改記另一首歌曲或作品，你對前兩個問題的回答會改變嗎？
  4. 你會如何向別人解釋自己的記譜系統？

Key Takeaways

  • The term “Western musical notation” refers to written symbols that are used to communicate musical sounds.
  • Western musical notation is one of many different systems of musical notation that appear across time spans, places, and cultures.
  • Someone who writes down music chooses a musical notation system based on these factors, also known as their social context or milieu.

Music is an auditory or aural art form that can be represented visually with notation.

Think about what it’s like to translate one language to another. It can be a challenging task—a direct word-for-word translation isn’t always possible or useful, so a translator needs to think about how best to communicate to the reader. Similarly, writing down sounds involves making decisions about what needs to be communicated and how. No system of notation could capture every aspect of sound in full detail and still be useful or readable, so only some musical elements can be depicted.

Despite this, there are several advantages to written musical notation. For example, a written musical work does not need to be memorized, and details can be recorded that might otherwise be forgotten. Additionally, written notation makes musical works accessible to more people, since anyone who knows the notation system can learn a notated work on their own without the help of a teacher.

Imagine inventing a way to write down a favorite song or musical work so that a musician could perform it without ever having heard it before. In the assignment below (“Inventing a Notation System”), you’ll have the opportunity to do this for a specific musical work or song of your choice. Here are some questions to consider as you listen to and think about creating your own musical notation system:

  1. What are the more important musical features of the work? What are the less important musical features?
  2. What are the musical features that you would want to write down? Are there any that you would leave out?
  3. Would your answers to questions 1 and 2 change if you were writing down a different song or work?
  4. How would you explain your notation system to someone else?
