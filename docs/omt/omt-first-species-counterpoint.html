第一類對位法

Mark Gotham and Kris Shaffer

重點整理

  • 第一類對位法是一種傳統的作曲練習，讓初學者學習如何安排旋律的起始與結束，更重要的是，如何保持各條旋律的獨立性。
  • 寫作第一類對位法時，請遵守下列規則：
    • 以完全一度、完全五度或完全八度開始。
    • 兩個聲部以完全相同的速率進行，沒有節奏變化，例如全部使用全音符。
    • 在和聲方面，兩聲部之間的音程全部為協和音程。
    • 在旋律方面，以級進為主，偶爾才用跳進。禁止使用三全音或七度的旋律跳進。
    • 禁止平行的完全協和音程。
    • 以完全一度或完全八度結束。
  • 本章還會介紹其他原則，幫助你寫好第一類對位法。

對位法將兩條以上的旋律協調成有意義且悅耳的整體。在第一類對位法中，我們要寫出流暢的旋律，使它本身具有完整的形狀、變化，以及朝向目標的進行；另一條旋律也要具備這些特質。更重要的是，兩條旋律結合後，整體織度也必須流暢、有變化、有方向感，而且各自保持獨立，同時又在每個同時發聲的位置融合成協和的音響。「同時發聲」（simultaneity）是兩個以上音符同時響起的通稱。
寫作第一類對位法時，先有一條定旋律，可以自行新作，也可以採用既有旋律，再在它的上方或下方寫出一條新旋律，稱為對位聲部。定旋律的每個音都對應新聲部的一個音；兩個聲部一律使用全音符。因此，第一類對位法也稱為「一音對一音」或 1:1 對位法。

Key Takeaways

  • First-species counterpoint is a traditional compositional exercise that teaches beginning musicians to consider how to start and end melodic lines, and most importantly, how to keep them independent of each other.
  • When writing in first species, follow these rules:
    • Begin on a perfect unison, fifth, or octave.
    • Both voices move at exactly the same rate and have no rhythmic variety (for example, all notes are whole notes).
    • Harmonically, the intervals between the two voices are all consonances.
    • Melodically, prefer stepwise motion and leap only occasionally. Melodic leaps of a tritone or seventh are forbidden.
    • Parallel perfect consonances are forbidden.
    • End with a perfect unison or octave.
  • This chapter presents additional guidelines that will help in writing a successful first-species counterpoint.

Counterpoint is the mediation of two or more musical lines into a meaningful and pleasing whole. In first-species counterpoint, we not only write a smooth melody that has its own integrity of shape, variety, and goal-directed motion, but we also write a second melody that contains these traits. Further, and most importantly, we combine these melodies to create a whole texture that is smooth, that exhibits variety and goal-oriented motion, and in which these melodies both maintain their independence and fuse together into consonant simultaneities (the general term for two or more notes sounding at the same time).
In first-species counterpoint, we begin with a cantus firmus (new or existing) and compose a single new line—called the counterpoint—above or below it. That new line contains one note for every note in the cantus: both the cantus firmus and the counterpoint will be all whole notes. Thus, first species is sometimes called "one-against-one" or 1:1 counterpoint.

對位聲部的旋律

大致而言，對位聲部應遵循前一章〈類種對位法入門〉所述的良好定旋律寫作原則。下文會說明幾個細微的差異，但總的來說，第一類對位法的兩條旋律都應達到良好定旋律的標準。

The Counterpoint Line

In general, the counterpoint should follow the principles of writing a good cantus firmus discussed in the previous chapter, Introduction to Species Counterpoint. There are some minor differences, to be discussed below, but generally a first-species counterpoint should consist of two cantus-firmus-quality lines.

Beginning and Ending

第一類對位法的起始

請注意，範例 1 中的每道練習都以完全協和音程開始，讓旋律一開始就有安定感。

若對位聲部寫在定旋律的上方，第一個音應為 do [latex](\hat1)[/latex] 或 sol [latex](\hat5)[/latex]，也就是與定旋律構成完全一度（P1），或在其上方的完全五度（P5）、完全八度（P8）。

若對位聲部寫在定旋律的下方，第一個音必須是調式終音 do [latex](\hat1)[/latex]，也就是與定旋律同音，或低一個完全八度。如果從 sol [latex](\hat5)[/latex] 開始，會構成不協和的四度；從 fa [latex](\hat4)[/latex] 開始雖能構成完全五度，卻會讓聽者混淆調性，因為樂曲開頭的 fa–do [latex](\hat4-\hat1)[/latex] 很容易被誤聽成 do–sol [latex](\hat1-\hat5)[/latex]。

譯註：本章以 do 代表調式終音；sol、fa 等唱名依本教材的首調唱名慣例理解。上、下聲部起始條件不同，不能把「可用五度」一概套到定旋律下方。

Beginning a first-species counterpoint

Note that each exercise in Example 1 begins with a perfect consonance. This creates a sense of stability at the opening of the line.

When writing a counterpoint above a cantus firmus, the first note of the counterpoint should be do [latex](\hat1)[/latex] or sol [latex](\hat5)[/latex] (a P1, P5, or P8 above the cantus).

When writing a counterpoint below a cantus firmus, the first note of the counterpoint must always be on the modal final, do [latex](\hat1)[/latex] (P1 or P8 below the cantus firmus). Beginning on sol [latex](\hat5)[/latex] would create a dissonant fourth; beginning on fa [latex](\hat4)[/latex] would create a P5 but confuse listeners about the tonal context, since fa–do [latex](\hat4-\hat1)[/latex] at the beginning of a piece is easily misheard as do–sol [latex](\hat1-\hat5)[/latex].

第一類對位法的結束

對位聲部的最後一個音必須是 do [latex](\hat1)[/latex]，也就是與定旋律同音，或高、低一個完全八度。

為了流暢地進入結尾，同時具有變化與明確的方向感，最後的音程一律以反向級進到達，方式如下：

  • 若定旋律以 re–do [latex](\hat2-\hat1)[/latex] 結束，對位聲部的最後兩個音應為 ti–do [latex](\hat7-\hat1)[/latex]。
  • 若定旋律以 ti–do [latex](\hat7-\hat1)[/latex] 結束，對位聲部的最後兩個音應為 re–do [latex](\hat2-\hat1)[/latex]。

因此，倒數第二小節中，兩條旋律之間會構成三度或六度（範例 2）。這種結尾公式稱為 clausula vera 終止式。範例 1 中的每道練習都以這種終止式結束。

Ending a first-species counterpoint

The final note of the counterpoint must always be do [latex](\hat1)[/latex] (P1 or P8 above/below the cantus).

To approach this ending smoothly, with variety, and with strong goal orientation, always approach the final interval by contrary stepwise motion, as follows:

  • If the cantus ends re–do [latex](\hat2-\hat1)[/latex], the counterpoint’s final two pitches should be ti–do [latex](\hat7-\hat1)[/latex].
  • If the cantus ends ti–do [latex](\hat7-\hat1)[/latex], the counterpoint’s final two pitches should be re–do [latex](\hat2-\hat1)[/latex].

Thus, the penultimate bar will either be a third or a sixth between the two lines (Example 2). This ending formula is known as the clausula vera. The exercises in Example 1 each end with a clausula vera.

各聲部的獨立性

與定旋律一樣，對位聲部也應只有一個高潮。為了保持各聲部的獨立性，以及整個樂段的流暢，兩者的高潮不應同時出現，避免雙重高潮使某一瞬間受到過度強調。〔原文段末另有孤立的術語連結：倒數第二個（penultimate）。〕

對位聲部容許有一次同音重複或延音線連結，但仍應盡量完全避免重複。練習原本就只有少數音符，這樣才能增加變化。

避免聲部交叉。交叉會削弱各聲部的獨立性，讓聽者更難分辨它們。

避免聲部超越，也就是某聲部越過另一聲部前一個音的音高。例如，若上聲部唱 E4，下聲部就不能在下一小節唱 F4。這也有助於保持各聲部的獨立性。

Independence of the Lines

Like the cantus firmus, the counterpoint line should have a single climax. To maintain the independence of the lines and the smoothness of the entire passage (so no one moment is hyper-emphasized by a double climax), these climaxes should not coincide.penultimate

A single repeat/tie in the counterpoint is allowed, but try to avoid repeating at all. This promotes variety in the exercise, since there are so few notes to begin with.

Avoid voice crossing. Voice crossings diminish the independence of the lines and make them more difficult to distinguish by ear.

Avoid voice overlap, where one voice leaps past the previous note of the other voice. For example, if the upper part sings an E4, the lower part cannot sing an F4 in the following bar. This also helps maintain the independence of the lines.

音程與聲部進行

在任何時刻，定旋律與對位聲部之間的音程都不應超過完全十二度，也就是八度加五度。大致而言，盡可能讓兩條旋律保持在八度以內；只有在不得已時才超過十度，而且只能維持一、兩個音。兩個聲部相距太遠，會減弱音響的融合，也可能降低演出的可行性。演奏是否方便雖然不是人類認知的基本原理，卻是作曲家必須考量的事，並且會直接影響聽者在演出中感受到的流暢度、旋律完整性與音響融合程度。

原則上，所有和聲協和音程都可以使用。不過，同度只能用在第一個和最後一個音程。同度非常穩定，較適合當作抵達的目標，而非途中的停留點；它也會削弱各聲部的獨立性。

除了開頭與結尾的兩音組合，其餘位置應優先採用不完全協和音程，少用完全協和音程，以加強結尾的抵達感，以及朝向結尾推進的感覺。無論如何，整道練習中的和聲音程都應有變化。

絕不可連續使用兩個同類的完全協和音程，例如 P5–P5 或 P8–P8。這項限制涵蓋單音程及其複音程；例如 P5–P12 視同 P5–P5。連續使用兩個不同類的完全協和音程，例如 P8–P5，則可以，但仍應盡可能在每個完全協和音程之後接上不完全協和音程。平行五度與八度雖會加強音響融合，卻犧牲了旋律的獨立性；連續的穩定音響也會限制練習的變化與動態。因此，它們並不理想，在類種對位法中應予避免。

譯註：本段原文的限制比「禁止平行五度／八度」更嚴格：它禁止相鄰出現同類完全協和音程，並明列 P5–P12 也算同類。翻譯保留本章第一類對位練習的規則，沒有自行放寬為只禁止同方向移動。

相鄰音程之間的聲部進行應有所變化，盡量用到各種類型，或許只有斜向進行可以例外。反向進行最能增加變化並保持各聲部的獨立性，因此應盡可能優先採用。

同向進行與平行進行會減少變化及旋律的獨立性，使用時應兼顧以下限制：

  • 同一類不完全協和音程最多只能連續使用三次，例如連續三個三度。
  • 絕不以同向進行到達完全協和音程；這種進行稱為直接五度／八度。它會使原本就已突出的音程受到過多關注。
  • 避免把同向進行與跳進結合，尤其應避免大跳。
譯註：這是本章嚴格的兩聲部練習規則；同向到達完全協和音程在其他寫作風格中可能有不同條件，不宜直接當作所有音樂的通則。

Intervals and Motion

The interval between the cantus firmus and counterpoint at any moment should not exceed a perfect twelfth (octave plus fifth). In general, try to keep the two lines within an octave where possible, and only exceed a tenth in “emergencies” and only briefly (one or two notes). When the voices are too far apart, tonal fusion is diminished. Further, it can diminish performability, which, though not an essential principle of human cognition, is an important consideration for composers, and it has a direct effect on the smoothness, melodic integrity, and tonal fusion of what listeners hear during a performance.

In general, all harmonic consonances are allowed. However, unisons should only be used for the first and last intervals. Unisons are very stable and serve best as goals rather than midpoints. They also diminish the independence of the lines.

Imperfect consonances are preferable to perfect consonances for all intervals other than the first and last dyads, in order to heighten the sense of arrival at the end and to promote a sense of motion toward that arrival. In all cases, aim for a variety of harmonic intervals over the course of the exercise.

Never use two perfect consonances of the same size in a row: P5–P5 or P8–P8. This includes both simple and compound intervals; for example, P5–P12 is considered the same as P5–P5. (Two different perfect consonances in a row, such as P8–P5, are allowed, but try to follow every perfect consonance with an imperfect consonance if possible.) Parallel fifths and octaves promote tonal fusion at the expense of melodic independence, and these consecutive stable sonorities also limit variety and motion in the exercise. Thus, they are far from ideal, and are to be avoided in species counterpoint.

Vary the types of motion between successive intervals, aiming to use each type (except perhaps oblique motion). Contrary motion is best for variety and preserving the independence of the lines, so it should be preferred where possible.

Because similar and parallel motion diminish variety and melodic independence, their use should be mediated by other factors:

  • Do not use more than three of the same imperfect consonance type in a row (e.g., three thirds in a row).
  • Never move into a perfect consonance by similar motion (this is called direct fifths/octaves. This draws too much attention to an interval that already stands out of the texture.
  • Avoid combining similar motion with leaps, especially large ones.
