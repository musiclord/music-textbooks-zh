以終止四六和弦加強結尾

John Peterson

重點整理

  • 本章介紹終止四六和弦 [latex]\mathrm{cadential}^6_4[/latex]，簡寫為 [latex](\mathrm{cad.}^6_4)[/latex]。它是屬和聲的裝飾，由低音 sol（[latex]\hat{5}[/latex]）上方的六度與四度兩個裝飾音結合而成。我們把 [latex]\mathrm{cad.\ }^6_4[/latex] 與它解決到 V(7) 的過程合併標記為 [latex]\mathrm{V}\begin{smallmatrix}(8-7)\\6-5\\4-3\end{smallmatrix}[/latex]。
  • 通常能接到 V 的和弦，也都能接到 [latex]\mathrm{cad. }^6_4[/latex]；最常見的是強屬前和弦。
  • 解決 [latex]\mathrm{cad.}^6_4[/latex] 時，須依數字指示，讓低音上方的六度下行為五度，四度下行為三度。

本章播放清單

前面已看到，屬和弦可以加上七音來強化，尤其常用於正格終止；半終止與正格終止也常藉由強屬前和弦加強。本章介紹另一種強化屬和聲解決動力的方法：終止 [latex]^6_4[/latex]，簡寫為 [latex](\mathrm{cad.}^6_4)[/latex]。

範例 1 的正格終止，包含以 [latex]\mathrm{cad.}^6_4[/latex] 裝飾的 V7。這裡刻意使用「裝飾」一詞，因為 [latex]\mathrm{cad.}^6_4[/latex] 是低音 sol（[latex]\hat{5}[/latex]）上方兩個裝飾音的組合。範例 1 使用的是一個經過音與一個掛留音。它們總是位於低音上方的六度與四度，出現時常加強我們對終止的期待，因此稱為 [latex]\mathrm{cad.}^6_4[/latex]。雖然 [latex]\mathrm{cad.}^6_4[/latex] 常出現在終止位置，也可能在樂句其他位置出現，作為 V(7) 的裝飾。

關於 [latex]^6_4[/latex] 和弦

[latex]^6_4[/latex] 和弦的特別之處，在於它包含與低音不協和的四度。因此，作曲家會以特殊方式處理 [latex]^6_4[/latex] 和弦，可分為四類。為了反映這些特定用法，每一類 [latex]^6_4[/latex] 都有與其功能相關的標籤。後續章節會介紹其餘 [latex]^6_4[/latex] 類型。

Key Takeaways

  • This chapter introduces the [latex]\mathrm{cadential}^6_4[/latex] [latex](\mathrm{cad.}^6_4)[/latex], an embellishment of the dominant that results from the combination of two embellishing tones a sixth and a fourth above the bass note sol ([latex]\hat{5}[/latex]). We label the [latex]\mathrm{cad.\ }^6_4[/latex] and its resolution to V(7) as one unit: [latex]\mathrm{V}\begin{smallmatrix}(8-7)\\6-5\\4-3\end{smallmatrix}[/latex].
  • Any chord that normally approaches V can approach [latex]\mathrm{cad. }^6_4[/latex]. Most commonly, this is one of the strong predominants.
  • When resolving [latex]\mathrm{cad.}^6_4[/latex], be sure to follow the figures such that the sixth above the bass falls to a fifth above the bass and the fourth above the bass falls to a third above the bass.

Chapter Playlist

So far, we've seen that the dominant can be strengthened, particularly at authentic cadences, by the addition of a seventh. We also saw that both half cadences and authentic cadences are commonly strengthened using a strong predominant. In this chapter, we look at another way to strengthen the the dominant’s drive toward resolution: the cadential [latex]^6_4[/latex] [latex](\mathrm{cad.}^6_4)[/latex].

The authentic cadence in Example 1 involves a V7 that has been embellished by [latex]\mathrm{cad.}^6_4[/latex]. We use the word “embellished” intentionally here because the [latex]\mathrm{cad.}^6_4[/latex] comprises two embellishing tones that appear over sol ([latex]\hat{5}[/latex]) in the bass. In Example 1, the embellishing tones are a passing tone and a suspension. These embellishing tones happen to always be a sixth and a fourth above the bass, and their appearance often intensifies the expectation to hear a cadence, hence the name “[latex]\mathrm{cad.}^6_4[/latex].” Although the [latex]\mathrm{cad.}^6_4[/latex] often shows up at cadence points, it may show up anywhere in a phrase as an embellishment of V(7).

Example 1. [latex]cad.\mathit{^6_4}[/latex] in Joseph Boulogne's String Quartet no. 4, I, mm. 45–47 (1:26–1:30).

A note on [latex]^6_4[/latex] chords.

[latex]^6_4[/latex] chords are special because they involve a dissonance (the fourth) with the bass. Composers therefore treat [latex]^6_4[/latex] chords in distinct ways, which fall into four categories. To acknowledge their special usage, each variety of [latex]^6_4[/latex] chord has its own label that relates to how the chord functions. Future chapters will introduce the remaining [latex]^6_4[/latex] chord types.

終止四六和弦的標記

你可能注意到，範例 1 的 [latex]\mathrm{cad.}^6_4[/latex] 包含 B[latex]\flat[/latex]、G、E[latex]\flat[/latex]，在片段的調性中，正好拼成主三和弦的第二轉位。為什麼卻標成 [latex]\mathrm{V}^6_4[/latex]？除了 [latex]\mathrm{cad.}^6_4[/latex] 是兩個裝飾音結合、並非獨立三和弦之外，還有兩個理由支持使用 [latex]\mathrm{V}^6_4[/latex]，而非 I[latex]^6_4[/latex]：

  1. 這個和弦出現在強屬前和弦之後。若標為 [latex]\mathrm{I}^6_4[/latex]，就暗示屬前功能走向主功能；但低音為 sol（[latex]\hat{5}[/latex]）時，我們並不會聽到這種進行。
  2. [latex]\mathrm{V}^6_4[/latex] 反映它聽起來是 V 的延伸裝飾；I[latex]^6_4[/latex] 則只反映音符的拼寫。[1]

Labeling cadential 6/4

You might have noticed that the [latex]\mathrm{cad.}^6_4[/latex] in Example 1 involves the notes B[latex]\flat[/latex], G, and E[latex]\flat[/latex], which spells a tonic triad in second inversion in the excerpt’s key. Why are we labeling this chord [latex]\mathrm{V}^6_4[/latex], then? Besides the fact that [latex]\mathrm{cad.}^6_4[/latex] arises from the combination of two embellishing tones (and therefore isn't a standalone triad), here are two additional reasons to use the label [latex]\mathrm{V}^6_4[/latex] over I[latex]^6_4[/latex]:

  1. The chord appears after a strong predominant. If we label it [latex]\mathrm{I}^6_4[/latex], we'd be implying that a predominant goes to tonic, which is not the sound we hear, given that sol ([latex]\hat{5}[/latex]) is in the bass.
  2. [latex]\mathrm{V}^6_4[/latex] reflects the chord’s sound as an elaboration of V, whereas I[latex]^6_4[/latex] reflects the chord’s spelling only.[1]

Writing with cadential 6/4

以四聲部寫出終止四六和弦

寫出 [latex]\mathrm{cad.}^6_4[/latex] 可依以下步驟，見範例 2：

  1. 低音寫 sol（[latex]\hat{5}[/latex]）。
  2. 找出低音上方六度與四度的音，選其中一個放在最高聲部，另一個稍後放入內聲部。
  3. 補入內聲部：一個聲部重複低音；在 [latex]\mathrm{cad.}^6_4[/latex] 中，為避免平行，這是必要的安排。另一個聲部採用第二步尚未使用的音。

Spelling cadential 6/4 in four voices

To spell [latex]\mathrm{cad.}^6_4[/latex], do the following (Example 2):

  1. Write sol ([latex]\hat{5}[/latex]) in the bass
  2. Determine what notes are a sixth and fourth above the bass. Choose one of those notes to place in the soprano. The other will go in an inner voice in step 3.
  3. Fill in the inner voices: one voice will double the bass, which is a necessity in [latex]\mathrm{cad.}^6_4[/latex] to avoid parallels. The other will take the unused note from step 2.

Voice leading with cadential 6/4

解決

終止 [latex]^6_4[/latex] 可以解決到 V 三和弦，見範例 3a、3c；也可以解決到 V7，見範例 3b、3d。標記 [latex]^{6-5}_{4-3}[/latex] 中的連線，說明 [latex]\mathrm{cad.}^6_4[/latex] 如何解決，也就是「這個進行必須保留在同一聲部中」。低音上方六度所在的聲部，應向下走到五度；四度所在的聲部，應向下走到三度。

加入七音同樣簡單：原本重複低音的聲部，向下級進成為和弦七音。數字 8–7 表示這項移動，即低音上方八度降到七度。

Resolution

Cadential [latex]^6_4[/latex] can resolve either to a V triad (Examples 3a, 3c) or a V7 chord (Examples 3b, 3d). The lines in the label [latex]^{6-5}_{4-3}[/latex] tell you how the [latex]\mathrm{cad.}^6_4[/latex] resolves, indicating “keep this motion in the same voice.” That is, whichever voice has a sixth above the bass should fall to a fifth above the bass, and whichever voice has the fourth above the bass should fall to a third above the bass.

Adding a seventh is just as straightforward: whatever voice is doubling the bass moves down a step to take the seventh of the chord. This motion is reflected by the figures 8-7 (the octave above the bass moves down to a seventh above the bass).

如何接近終止四六和弦

[latex]\mathrm{cad.}^6_4[/latex] 是屬和聲的裝飾，因此任何能接到 V 的和聲，也能接到 [latex]\mathrm{cad.}^6_4[/latex]。最常見的仍是強屬前和弦 IV 與 ii6，如範例 4。

這裡有兩項原則：

  1. 和處理其他屬前區域時一樣，要留意屬前和弦與 [latex]\mathrm{cad.}^6_4[/latex] 之間的平行八度。
  2. 進入與離開 [latex]\mathrm{cad.}^6_4[/latex] 通常都很平順，應避免跳進到 [latex]\mathrm{cad.}^6_4[/latex] 的和弦成員。作曲家偶爾會跳進到低音上方的六度，但跳到四度的情況少得多，因為四度不協和，因此尤其應避免。

Approaching cadential 6/4

Since the [latex]\mathrm{cad.}^6_4[/latex] embellishes the dominant, any harmony that approaches V can also approach [latex]\mathrm{cad.}^6_4[/latex]. Most commonly, though, these are the strong predominants IV and ii6 (Example 4).

Two guidelines apply here:

  1. As always when dealing with the predominant area, watch out for parallel octaves between the predominant and [latex]\mathrm{cad.}^6_4[/latex].
  2. Motion into (and out of) the [latex]\mathrm{cad.}^6_4[/latex] is usually very smooth. Avoid leaping to a member of the [latex]\mathrm{cad.}^6_4[/latex]. While composers do occasionally leap to the sixth above the bass, it's comparatively much rarer to leap to the fourth above the bass because it’s a dissonance, so that in particular should be avoided.
