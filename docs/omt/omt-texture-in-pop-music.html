流行音樂的織體

Megan Lavengood

重點整理

  • 功能層是依樂器在整體織體中扮演的角色，將它們分組的方法。
  • 流行歌曲中的功能層包括：
    1. 明示拍節層：顧名思義，清楚呈現歌曲的拍節與各脈動層次。多數打擊樂屬於這一層。
    2. 功能低音層：為歌曲提供和聲基礎。低音域樂器很可能屬於這一層。
    3. 和聲填充層：增加織體厚度，提供更多和聲脈絡。通常由鍵盤、合成器與吉他構成，不同曲風的配置可能相差很大。
    4. 旋律層：通常由人聲及其他旋律樂器演奏。特色是音色變化靈活，且相較周圍織體，樂器音色較明亮。
    5. 新奇層：透過呼應或插入式音型，與旋律層形成對照。這一層常使用音色不容易與其他樂器融合的樂器。

本章播放清單

尤其在音序器與數位音訊工作站出現以後，以分層方式構思、寫作的流行音樂，所占比例越來越高。這種分層作曲方式是流行音樂的重要結構特徵，值得仔細分析。Allan Moore（2012）指出，傳統描述織體的術語，例如主調織度、異音織度、複調織度與單音織度，原本是為描述古典音樂而發展，套用在流行音樂上，能提供的洞見有限。[1]他因此提出功能層的概念，作為更有解釋力的流行音樂織體分析工具。一個功能層可以只對應一件樂器，也可以由一組樂器共同構成。

譯註：織體也稱織度；四種傳統分類沿用本書基礎章的單音、異音、主調與複調織度名稱。功能層是另一種分析視角，並不表示傳統分類在流行音樂中完全無法使用。

Moore（2012）提出流行音樂常見的四個功能層：

  1. 明示拍節層
  2. 功能低音層
  3. 和聲填充層
  4. 旋律層

Lavengood（2020）加入第五層：

  1. 新奇層

辨識這些功能層看似不複雜，但分析各層如何構成，是拆解歌曲、加深理解的一種方式。功能層分析也能顯示歌曲中，與特定曲風或文化相關的常見做法。

本章以Vulfpeck的〈3 on E〉（2020）說明這些概念。例1列出各功能層的樂器，例2則用分層圖，呈現這些樂器在全曲中的使用情形。每件樂器的顏色，都對應它所屬的功能層。

Key Takeaways

  • A functional layer is a way of grouping instruments together according to the role the instruments play within the larger texture.
  • Some functional layers in pop songs include:
    1. The explicit beat layer, which (as the name suggests) clearly articulates the meter and pulse levels within the song. Most percussion is part of this layer.
    2. The functional bass layer, which provides a harmonic foundation for the track. Bass-register instruments likely belong to in this layer.
    3. The harmonic filler layer, which thickens the texture and provides more harmonic context to the song. This layer typically comprises keyboards, synthesizers, and guitars, and varies widely depending on genre.
    4. The melodic layer, which is typically performed by the voice and other melodic instruments. The melodic layer is notable for involving high timbral flexibility, as well as instruments that are bright relative to the surrounding texture.
    5. The novelty layer, which works in opposition to the melodic layer through call-and-response gestures or interjections. This layer often uses instruments whose timbral features resist blending with the rest of the ensemble.

Chapter Playlist

Especially since the advent of sequencers and digital audio workstations, an ever-increasing majority of popular music is conceptualized and written in layers. This layered approach to composition is an important structural feature of pop music, deserving of close analytical attention. Allan Moore (2012) observes that traditional terms for describing texture (homophony, heterophony, polyphony, monophony) were developed to describe classical music, and do not offer much insight when applied to pop music.[1] He developed the concept of functional layers as a more powerful tool for describing pop textures. Functional layers may be equivalent to a single instrument, or a group of instruments may constitute a single functional layer.

Moore (2012) identifies four functional layers common in pop music:

  1. The explicit beat layer
  2. The functional bass layer
  3. The harmonic filler layer
  4. The melodic layer

Lavengood (2020) adds a fifth layer:

  1. The novelty layer

While identifying these layers may not seem like a complex task, analyzing the way they are constituted is one way of breaking down a song to understand it better. Analyzing functional layers may furthermore illuminate common practices in the song associated with specific genres and/or cultures.

The concepts in this chapter will be illustrated with the song "3 on E" by Vulfpeck (2020). A list of the instruments that constitute each functional layer is given in Example 1, and the use of the instruments throughout the song is diagrammed in a layer graph in Example 2. Colors assigned to the instruments reflect their functional layer.

明示拍節層

這一層明確呈現拍點的規律模式（Moore 2012，20）。它也是歌曲律動的重要部分，負責建立、維持拍節與特色節奏。這一層常由無固定音高的打擊樂器構成；許多流行音樂主要以鼓組呈現明示拍節層，當然也能加入其他打擊樂器。由於許多流行音樂是為跳舞而作，這一層的節奏通常規律、反覆。因此，它為歌曲其他部分提供基礎，而其他部分在節奏上可以更自由。

這些基本特性，讓明示拍節層在流行歌曲中特別容易辨認。〈3 on E〉幾乎全曲都有這一層，由鼓組演奏。最後一次副歌（2:20）又加入輔助打擊樂器。上方例2以紫色呈現明示拍節層。

Explicit Beat Layer

This layer "articulates an explicit pattern of beats" (Moore 2012, 20). It is also a key component to the song's groove, as it establishes and maintains the song's meter and characteristic rhythms. Instruments in this layer are often unpitched percussion instruments; in much pop music, the drum kit is the instrument that primarily articulates the explicit beat layer, though of course other percussion may be added. The rhythms performed in the explicit beat layer also tend to be regular and repetitive, since so much pop music is meant to be danced to. The explicit beat layer thus provides a foundation for the rest of the track, which is more flexible in this regard.

These fundamental properties of the explicit beat layer render it particularly easy to recognize in pop songs. In "3 on E," the explicit beat layer is present throughout almost the entire track and is played by the drum kit. In the final chorus (2:20), additional auxiliary percussion in the form is added to the explicit beat layer. Example 2 above shows the explicit beat layer with purple colors.

功能低音層

和明示拍節層一樣，功能低音層也是歌曲律動的重要部分，共同構成歌曲的基礎。注意上方例2的藍色功能低音層，也和明示拍節層一樣，幾乎貫穿全曲。不同之處是，功能低音層還肩負音域與調性上的重要角色：提供低頻，並界定歌曲的和聲脈絡。絕大多數流行歌曲中，功能低音層在每次換和弦時的第1拍演奏和弦根音，同時滿足這兩個角色（例3）。

譯註：這裡描述常見做法，不代表每首流行歌曲都只在小節第1拍換和弦，或低音一定只奏根音。功能低音層是按角色分類，不能單憑樂器名稱決定。

Functional Bass Layer

Like the explicit beat layer, the functional bass layer is an important component of the song's groove, and forms part of the foundation of a track. Note that in Example 2 above, the functional bass (shown in blue) is present throughout almost the entire song, like the explicit beat layer. Unlike the explicit beat layer, the functional bass layer also serves an important registral and tonal role: to provide the bass frequency and define the harmonic context of a song. In the vast majority of pop songs, the functional bass layer fills these two roles at once by playing the chord roots on beat 1 of each chord change (Example 3).

旋律層

歌曲的主要旋律線構成旋律層。人聲自然是其中最典型的樂器，也最不需要額外的理論說明。其他非人聲樂器之所以歸入旋律層，是因為它們承擔旋律線，與人聲一起演奏，或取代人聲。上方例2以紅色呈現旋律層；人聲是典型例子，偶爾也有銅管音色的FM合成器加入。就像這個合成器，旋律層的樂器通常比織體其他部分具有更明亮的音色，而且可能有高度的音色變化能力，模仿歌唱人聲的變化。Moore也指出，旋律層的配器，是辨認歌曲曲風的重要線索。

Melodic layer

The principal melodic line(s) of a song form the melodic layer. Of course, the voice is the most typical instrument in the melodic layer, and requires the least theorizing. When other, non-vocal instruments belong to the melodic layer, it is because they carry the melodic line, either alongside or instead of the voice. In Example 2 above, the vocals are a paradigmatic example of the melodic layer (shown in red), and occasionally a brassy FM synth joins the vocals in the melodic layer. As with this brassy synth, instruments in the melodic layer tend to have brighter timbres than those of instruments in other parts of the texture, and moreover, may have a high degree of timbral flexibility that mimics similar changes in the singing voice. Moore also points out that the instrumentation of the melodic layer is a strong indicator of the genre the song belongs to.

和聲填充層

Moore說，和聲填充層填補低音層與旋律層之間的「音域」空間（2012，21）。雖然其中確實可能包含中頻樂器，但定義中的引號提示，這裡談的是概念上的音域空間，而不是嚴格按頻率劃分的音域。吉他、鍵盤與合成器最常出現在和聲填充層，〈3 on E〉就是如此，見上方例2；不過，配置會隨曲風大幅改變，合唱團、管弦樂團或流行樂管樂組，也可能擔任這個功能。[2]

Harmonic filler layer

Moore says that the harmonic filler layer "fill[s] the 'registral' space between the bass and [melodic] layers" (2012, 21). While harmonic filler layers may indeed include mid-range instruments, the presence of scare quotes in Moore's definition indicates that this is about conceptual registral space, rather than strict frequency-based register. Guitars, keyboards, and synthesizers are most common in the harmonic filler layer (as in "3 on E," as shown in Example 2 above), but this can vary greatly depending on genre—choirs, orchestras, and horn sections could also be appropriate.[2]

新奇層

新奇層與旋律層形成對照，特色是呼應或插入式音型，以及不尋常的音色。它是本章功能層中最不具必要性的一層，不見得每首歌都有。搖滾樂標準配置以外的樂器，也就是吉他、鼓和鍵盤以外的樂器，以及吸引注意的合成器音色，常被用來構成新奇層。[3]〈3 on E〉第二次主歌（0:59）中的陶笛，同時符合這個定義在音型與音色上的條件，形成典型、但出現時間很短的新奇層；上方例2以黃色標示。其他明顯的新奇層例子，還有Jason Derulo〈Talk Dirty〉（2013，0:39）中的薩克斯風，以及Celine Dion〈My Heart Will Go On〉（1997，0:00）中的錫笛。

譯註：原文此處寫「藍色」，但同章例1表格、例2圖說與公開互動圖均把陶笛／新奇層標為黃色，藍色則屬功能低音層；繁中已依圖表一致修正。0:59等時間碼依原文保留，沒有以互動圖替代錄音聆聽核對。

Novelty layer

The novelty layer functions in opposition to the melodic layer, and is characterized by call-and-response or interjectory gestures and unusual timbres. It is the least essential of all the functional layers discussed in this chapter, and may not be present in every song. Non-standard rock instruments (that is, instruments beyond guitars, drums, and keyboards) and attention-grabbing synthesizer sounds often are used to create a novelty layer.[3] The ocarinas in the second verse of "3 on E" (0:59) fulfill both the gestural and timbral parameters of this definition, creating a paradigmatic (albeit minimally present) novelty layer (shown in blue in Example 2 above). Other examples with prominent novelty layers are the saxophone in "Talk Dirty" by Jason Derulo (2013, 0:39) or the tin whistle in "My Heart Will Go On" by Celine Dion (1997, 0:00).

分析時可以提出的問題

如同其他音樂分析，貼上標籤只是形成有說服力之分析的起點。以下問題，可以幫助你從辨識功能層，進一步發展成以織體為基礎的歌曲詮釋：

  • 各層由哪些樂器構成？
  • 各層何時進入、何時退出？這與曲式有什麼關係？
  • 是否有某個功能層完全缺席？省略它會產生什麼效果？
  • 新奇層的配器，暗示了哪些跨作品、跨文本的聯想？
  • 各層的節奏輪廓有何差異？
  • 是否有樂器參與不只一個功能層？角色改變會產生什麼效果？
  • 上述答案如何與曲風或文化脈絡相連？

Questions to ask while analyzing

As always with music analysis, applying labels is only the beginning of creating a compelling analysis. Below are some ideas for questions to ask yourself to develop your labelling of functional layers into an interpretation of the song based on texture:

  • What instruments make up each layer?
  • When do layers enter and exit, and how does that relate to the form?
  • Are any functional layers missing altogether? What is the effect of omitting that layer from the song?
  • What intertextual associations are implied by the instrumentation of the novelty layer?
  • How do the rhythmic profiles of the layers differ?
  • Do any instruments participate in multiple functional layers? What is the effect of their change of role?
  • How do any of the answers to the questions above relate to genre or cultural context?
