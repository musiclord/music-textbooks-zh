超小節拍節（新版）

Brian Jarvis

重點整理

  • 超小節拍節是一種由聽覺感知、而非直接記寫在譜上的拍節組織；在概念上，它位於一般拍節之上的層次。
  • 超小節拍節的清晰程度是一個連續光譜：有些音樂中的超小節拍節很弱，甚至不存在；另一些音樂，例如多數舞曲，則很強而且清楚。
  • 四拍是最常見的超小節拍節類型。
  • 不同超拍可能與特定音樂特徵相聯繫：
    • 第1超拍：與開始相關。
    • 第4超拍：與結束／終止式相關。
    • 第2、3超拍：與中段相關。
  • 規律的超小節拍節可能因重新解讀與重複而受到擾動。

Key Takeaways

  • Hypermeter is a perceived (non-notated) metric organization that is at a conceptually higher level than the regular meter.
  • Hypermeter exists on a continuum: in some music, it may be weak or non-existent; in other music (e.g., most dance music), it may be strong and clear.
  • Quadruple is the most common type of hypermeter.
  • Hyperbeats can have associations with musical features:
    • Hyperbeat 1: Associated with beginnings
    • Hyperbeat 4: Associated with endings/cadences
    • Hyperbeats 2 & 3: Associated with middles
  • A regular hypermeter can be disrupted by reinterpretation and repetition.

概述

字首「hyper-」意為「在……之上」或「超越」。超小節拍節是由聽覺感知、而非直接記寫在譜上的拍節組織，在概念上比一般拍節高一個層次。即使音樂已有樂譜，超小節拍節也不會直接記寫出來；當你察覺到比單一小節更長、且有規律的時間跨度時，便可能聽見它。

舞曲中通常可以找到明確的超小節拍節。例如蕾哈娜2012年的歌曲〈鑽石〉（Diamonds），以四小節循環的和聲進行G–Bmi–A–A呈現超小節拍節。和弦循環每次重新開始，就開啟一個新的超小節；可留意歌詞以「Find light」、「You're a shooting star」及「I knew」開頭等位置。例1最下排彩色弧線表示超小節，每個超小節都由四個超拍，也就是四個小節組成。

譯註：歌詞在此用來辨認超小節起點；中文保留縮短的英文辨識短語，並非提供整首歌詞的翻譯。Bmi表示B小三和弦。

超小節拍節和一般拍節一樣，可以是四拍、三拍或二拍：

  • 四拍：四個小節（超拍）＝一個超小節。
  • 三拍：三個小節（超拍）＝一個超小節。
  • 二拍：兩個小節（超拍）＝一個超小節。

四拍的超小節拍節最為常見，二拍較少，三拍則最少。聽者通常透過這段時間內發生的變化與／或重複，感知超小節的範圍。〈鑽石〉靠的是四小節和弦循環的重複，但還有其他方式：如果一段音樂包含四小節樂句，接著立即重複一次，就可以聽成兩個四拍超小節。同樣地，一個四小節樂句之後接上明顯對比的四小節樂句，也能形成兩個四拍超小節。

和一般拍節一樣，一首作品的超小節拍節大多保持一致，例如始終為四拍；但也可能出現不規則的長度。這通常來自樂句擴充手法，造成超小節拍節的擾動，下文會進一步討論。如果整首作品或整段音樂的長度始終十分不規則，就可能無法感知其中的超小節拍節。音樂也常有模糊之處，因此同一首作品可能容許不同的超小節拍節解讀。

Overview

The prefix "hyper-" means "over" or "above." Hypermeter is a perceived (non-notated) metric organization that is at a conceptually higher level above regular meters. Even in notated music, hypermeter is not notated—it is something you hear when you detect regular spans of time longer than a single measure.

Hypermeter is reliably found in dance music. For example, in Rihanna's 2012 song "Diamonds," the four-measure looping harmonic progression (G–Bmi–A–A) articulates the hypermeter. Each repetition of the chord loop begins a new hypermeasure—listen for them beginning on the lyrics "Find light in the beautiful ...", "You're a shooting star I see ...", "I knew that we'd become ...", and so on. In Example 1, the lowest row of colored arches shows the hypermeasures, each consisting of four hyperbeats (measures).

Open interactive example

Example 1. Hypermetric Analysis of Rihanna's "Diamonds" (2012). Click to view interactive example.

Like regular meters, hypermeters may be quadruple, triple, or duple:

  • Quadruple: four measures (hyperbeats) = one hypermeasure
  • Triple: three measures (hyperbeats) = one hypermeasure
  • Duple: two measures (hyperbeats) = one hypermeasure

Quadruple is by far the most common hypermeter, duple is less common, and triple is the least common. Hypermeasures are typically perceived due to changes and/or repetitions that take place during that span of time. In "Diamonds," this was the repetition of the four-bar chord loop, but there are other methods as well. If a passage contains a four-measure phrase and its immediate repetition, the passage can be heard as two quadruple hypermeasures. Similarly, a four-measure phrase followed by a distinctly contrasting four-measure phrase can produce two quadruple hypermeasures.

Like regular meter, hypermeter tends to be mostly consistent throughout a work (e.g., always quadruple), but irregular lengths can occur, typically due to phrase expansion techniques that result in hypermetric disruption, discussed more below. If lengths are very irregular throughout, a piece or a passage of music may not have a perceivable hypermeter. And as always, ambiguities often arise in music, and different interpretations of a piece's hypermeter are possible.

超拍與音樂特徵的關聯

聆聽一般拍節時，某些音樂特徵會與特定拍點相聯繫，幫助我們辨認自己位於拍節的哪個位置。例如和弦通常在第1拍改變；反拍重音則出現在第2與第4拍。超小節拍節也一樣：只要知道該聽什麼，往往就能辨認目前是哪一個超拍。對於四拍的超小節拍節，可以留意下列特徵：

  • 第1超拍：與開始相關。可能是新樂句或次樂句的開始，也可能是先前已聽過樂句的重複。
  • 第4超拍：與結束相關。在有終止式的音樂中，終止式通常出現在第4超拍。
  • 第2與第3超拍：與中段相關，通常不像第1與第4超拍那麼容易辨認。在有終止式的音樂中，第3超拍可能讓人感到音樂正朝第4超拍的終止式前進。

Hyperbeat Associations

When listening for regular meter, certain musical features are associated with certain beats, and this orients us within the meter. For example, chord changes usually happen on beat 1; a backbeat happens on beats 2 and 4. Similarly, in hypermeter, you can often hear which hyperbeat is happening if you know what to listen for. In quadruple hypermeter, you can listen for the following things:

  • Hyperbeat 1: Associated with beginnings. Can be the beginning of a new phrase or subphrase or it can be a repetition of one you've already heard.
  • Hyperbeat 4: Associated with endings. In music with cadences, the cadences typically happen in hyperbeat 4.
  • Hyperbeats 2 and 3: Associated with middles. These hyperbeats are often are not as easily identifiable as hyperbeats 1 and 4. In music with cadences, hyperbeat 3 may feel like it's leading toward the cadence in hyperbeat 4.

Rihanna, "Diamonds": Hypermeter and phrasing

We introduced hypermeter with "Diamonds" (Example 1) because of its clear, regular, quadruple hypermeter. But the way the phrasing lines up with the hypermeter is worth further attention.

Notice how in the verses (Example 2a), Rihanna's phrases start in the first hyperbeat of each hypermeasure, but they don't start on the metrical downbeat. But at the very beginning of each chorus (Example 2b), her lyrical phrase contains a pick-up that does lead into the simultaneous arrival of the metrical and hypermetrical downbeats. The alignment of metrical downbeat, hyperdownbeat, and melodic accent emphasize the arrival of the chorus.

The Beatles, "A Hard Day's Night" (1964): Hypermetric repetition

This song also contains quadruple hypermeter. To hear it, pay attention to the beginnings of each lyric phrase ("It's been a hard ...", "But when I get home ...", "When I'm home ..." ) and of each harmonic progression (I–IV–I–♭VII–I, or G–C–G–F–G, for the first two phrases). Notice that the quadruple hypermeter is very consistent throughout the song, except for the very last phrase.

In Example 3, the last phrase has the hypermetric counts [latex]\overbrace{1-2-3-4-\mathbf{3-4-3-4}}[/latex] where we might have expected another repetition of a regular [latex]1-2-3-4[/latex] hypermetric counting. In this last phrase, the music associated with the 3rd and 4th hyperbeats is repeating, which leads to a perception of repeating those two hyperbeats instead of starting over with the material for hyperbeat 1 (Hypermetric Repetition). To hear this interpretation, compare the last phrase of each A section and pay special attention to the lines that end with "feel all right" or "feel okay." They only happen one time per A section, but in the last one, it keeps repeating instead of moving on to something else like it had been doing.

Labeling the final phrases as two hypermeasures [latex](\overbrace{1-2-3-4}\quad\overbrace{1-2-3-4})[/latex] is also an accurate interpretation of the passage, but the advantage of labeling the repeated 3–4s is that the hypermetrical analysis better reflects the musical repetition.

It's likely to be different especially between the double-speed and half-speed versions:

  • Example 5: This is performed at the normal, notated tempo for this piece. You probably conduct a simple triple meter, as notated. You can likely feel the four-bar hypermeter easily as well.

Just as you can hear different levels of meter at these different tempos, you can hear different levels of meter even if the tempo does not change. The perceptual change in tactus you may experience is just your mind entraining different levels of meter.

Hypermetric Disruptions: reinterpretation and repetition

重新解讀

超小節拍節的重新解讀是一種超小節拍節擾動：同一個超拍同時具有兩種功能。它幾乎總是出現在樂句重疊處，也就是一個超小節的結尾（第4超拍）同時成為新超小節的開頭（第1超拍）。[1]分析時以等號表示：[latex]4=1[/latex]。例如，兩個超小節之間發生重新解讀，就可以寫成[latex]1-2-3-\mathbf{4=1}-2-3-4[/latex]。

以沃爾夫岡・莫札特《第21號鋼琴協奏曲》K. 467第一樂章（1785）為例。例8的第12小節，第一個樂句的結尾與第二個樂句的開頭相互重疊。請注意，發生超小節拍節重新解讀的小節標有[latex]4=1[/latex]。第三個超小節的音樂與第二個相同，因此第二個超小節也成了預期第三個如何進行的範本。請聽聽第12小節如何仍作為第一個樂句的結尾，而新樂句卻已同時接手，清楚帶出新超小節的開始。開啟互動範例

Reinterpretation

Hypermetric reinterpretation is a type of hypermetric disruption that occurs when a single hyperbeat is simultaneously functioning in two ways. Almost always, this occurs at a phrase elision, in which the end of one hypermeasure (hyperbeat 4) is simultaneously the beginning of a new hypermeasure (hyperbeat 1).[1] In an analysis, we notate this with an equals sign: [latex]4=1[/latex]. An example of two hypermeasures with reinterpretation would be [latex]1-2-3-\mathbf{4=1}-2-3-4[/latex].

As an example, take Wolfgang Mozart's Piano Concerto no. 21, K. 467, mvt. i (1785). In Example 8, in measure 12, the end of the first phrase elides with the beginning of the second phrase. Note that the notation [latex]4=1[/latex] in the measure where the hypermetric reinterpretation occurs. The music from the third hypermeasure is just like that of the second hypermeasure, so the second one functions as a model for how the third should go. Listen to how measure 12 still functions as the end of the first phrase, but the new phrase simultaneously takes over, providing a clear sense of the beginning of a new hypermeasure. 重複

超拍重複是另一種超小節拍節擾動，表示超小節擴充到超過原先預期的小節數，例如從四小節增為五小節。當你說自己聽見超拍重複，就表示你認為超小節內的一個或數個超拍立即重複了。例如，原本的[latex]1-2-3-4[/latex]可以重複第4超拍，成為[latex](1-2-3-4-\mathbf{4})[/latex]；也可以把第3與第4超拍一起重複，成為[latex](1-2-3-4-\mathbf{3-4})[/latex]，就像〈一夜狂歡〉那樣。當這段音樂是先前段落的變體時，超拍重複的解讀通常最有說服力，因為相似的音樂通常應採相似的解讀。超拍重複屬於超小節拍節的擾動。

在莫札特歌劇《唐喬望尼》的二重唱〈讓我們攜手同行〉（Là ci darem la mano）中，唐喬望尼唱出一個平行樂段；緊接著，采琳娜唱出基本相同的平行樂段，歌詞不同，而且這次最後一個樂句有所擴充。請比較兩人的後句：因為我們先聽到唐喬望尼的樂句，它就成了預期采琳娜如何演唱的範本。采琳娜的版本打破了這項預期，帶來輕微的音樂驚喜，將原本預期的四小節延長成六小節。例9把這項擴充標成超拍重複：第3、4超拍立即再出現一次。雖然稍有變化，它們的終止功能與整體效果仍十分相似。圖中的CAD表示原本預期的終止位置；這個終止式隨後被迴避，轉而在兩小節後提出並實現完全正格終止（PAC）。

Repetition

Hypermetric repetition is another type of hypermetric disruption that indicates a hypermeasure has been expanded beyond its expected number of measures (from four measures to five, for example). If you say you hear a hypermetric repetition, this means you think one or more hyperbeats within a hypermeasure have been immediately repeated. For example, instead of [latex]1-2-3-4[/latex], you could have hyperbeat 4 repeat [latex](1-2-3-4-\mathbf{4})[/latex], or 3 and 4 could repeat together [latex](1-2-3-4-\mathbf{3-4})[/latex] like in "Hard Day's Night." Hypermetric repetition is usually most convincing when the musical material in question is a variant of a previously stated passage, because similar music should normally be interpreted similarly. Hypermetric repetition is a type of hypermetric disruption.

In Mozart's duet "Là ci darem la mano" from Don Giovanni, Don Giovanni sings a parallel period; immediately after, Zerlina sings essentially the same parallel period (with different lyrics), but this time the last phrase is expanded. Compare Don Giovanni's consequent phrase with Zerlina's: because we hear Don Giovanni's phrase first, it serves as the model of what Zerlina's will sound like, and Zerlina's version then violates our expectations, providing a mild musical surprise that extends the phrase from the expected four measures to six. Example 9 shows the expansion as a hypermetric repetition where hyperbeats 3 and 4 are immediately repeated. They are slightly varied, but their cadential function and overall effect remain very similar. The notation CAD indicates the location of the cadence that was proposed and then evaded in favor the PAC that is proposed and accepted two measures later.
