織度

Samuel Brady and Chelsey Hamm

重點整理

  • 音樂的織度指作品中不同聲部的密度，以及聲部之間的互動。
  • 單音織度（monophony）的特徵是一條沒有伴奏的旋律線。
  • 異音織度（heterophony）的特徵是同一旋律線的多個變體同時發聲。
  • 主調織度（homophony）的特徵是多個聲部在和聲上以相同的步調共同推進。
  • 複調織度（polyphony）的特徵是多個聲部各有不同的旋律線與節奏。
  • 多數音樂並不一直維持單一織度，而會在不同織度之間轉換。

本章播放清單

織度是音樂中重要、卻有時被忽略的面向。音樂織度有許多類型，學者主要使用四種分類：單音、異音、主調與複調。

Key Takeaways

  • Musical texture is the density of and interaction between a work’s different voices.
  • Monophony is characterized by an unaccompanied melodic line.
  • Heterophony is characterized by multiple variants of a single melodic line heard simultaneously.
  • Homophony is characterized by multiple voices harmonically moving together at the same pace.
  • Polyphony is characterized by multiple voices with separate melodic lines and rhythms.
  • Most music does not conform to a single texture; rather, it can move between them.

Chapter Playlist

Texture is an important (and sometimes overlooked) aspect of music. There are many types of musical texture, but the four main categories used by music scholars are monophony, heterophony, homophony, and polyphony.

單音織度

單音織度的特徵是一條沒有伴奏的旋律線。所有參與的樂器或歌者都齊奏、齊唱同一旋律，因此它是最簡單、旋律也最直接呈露的織度。約翰・塞巴斯蒂安・巴赫《G 大調第一號無伴奏大提琴組曲》（1717）的第一樂章，就是單音織度的例子。請注意，大提琴獨奏旋律是此曲唯一的聲部。

聆聽：G 大調第一號大提琴組曲 BWV 1007，第一樂章〈前奏曲〉（Spotify）

例 1。約翰・塞巴斯蒂安・巴赫《G 大調第一號大提琴組曲》BWV 1007，第一樂章〈前奏曲〉，馬友友演奏。

接著聆聽 Pete Seeger 的〈Where Have All the Flowers Gone?〉（1955）。請注意，Seeger 的歌聲是唯一的音樂線條，因此這是單音織度的第二個例子。

聆聽：Where Have All the Flowers Gone? 現場版（Spotify）

例 2。Pete Seeger 的〈Where Have All the Flowers Gone?〉。

Monophony

A monophonic texture is characterized by a single unaccompanied melodic line of music. Monophony involves all instruments playing or singing in unison, making it the simplest and most exposed of all musical textures. The first movement of Cello Suite no. 1 in G Major (1717) by Johann Sebastian Bach is an example of a monophonic texture. Notice how the solo cello line is the only voice in this work.

Spotify Embed: Cello Suite No. 1 in G Major, BWV 1007: I. Prélude

Example 1. Cello Suite no. 1 in G Major, I. Prelude,  (BWV 1007) by Johann Sebastian Bach, performed by Yo-Yo Ma.

Now let’s listen to “Where Have All the Flowers Gone?” (1955) by Pete Seeger. Note that Seeger’s voice is the only musical line; therefore, this work is a second example of monophony.

Spotify Embed: Where Have All the Flowers Gone? - Live

Example 2. “Where Have All the Flowers Gone?” by Pete Seeger.

異音織度

異音織度的特徵是不同聲部同時呈現同一旋律線的多種變化。只要旋律材料仍相對一致，變化可以小至裝飾音，也可以大至某個聲部中較長的快速音群。

請聆聽 Göskel Baktagir 的〈Ana Hasreti〉（2001），這是一個土耳其古典音樂的例子。注意管樂如何裝飾撥弦樂器呈現的旋律。雖然各樂器演奏不同裝飾，基本旋律材料仍然相同。

聆聽：Ana Hasreti（Spotify）

例 3。Göskel Baktagir 的〈Ana Hasreti〉（2001）。

接著從 0:50 開始，聆聽 The Chieftains 於 1978 年錄製的愛爾蘭傳統里爾舞曲〈The Wind That Shakes the Barley〉。注意民間小提琴與長笛旋律線之間的細微差異；這些細微變化構成異音織度的第二個例子。

聆聽：The Wind That Shakes the Barley／The Reel With the Beryle（Spotify）

例 4。The Chieftains 演奏的〈The Wind That Shakes the Barley／The Reel With the Beryle〉；請從 0:50 開始聆聽。

Heterophony

A heterophonic texture is characterized by multiple variations of the same melodic line that are heard simultaneously across different voices. These variations can range from small embellishing tones to longer runs in a single voice, as long as the melodic material stays relatively constant.

Listen to “Ana Hasreti” (2001) by Göskel Baktagir, an example of Turkish classical music. Notice how the winds embellish the melody presented by the plucked strings. While the instruments play different embellishments, they present essentially the same melodic material.

Spotify Embed: Ana Hasreti

Example 3. “Ana Hasreti” (2001) by Göskel Baktagir.

Now listen to the traditional Irish reel “The Wind That Shakes the Barley,” recorded by the Chieftains in 1978 (beginning at 0:50), and notice the slight variation between the melodic lines of the fiddle (violin) and the flute. This slight variation between the violin and flute presents a second example of heterophony.

Spotify Embed: The Wind That Shakes The Barley/The Reel With The Beryle

Example 4. “The Wind That Shakes The Barley/The Reel With The Beryle” by The Chieftains; listen starting at 0:50.

主調織度

主調織度的特徵是多個聲部在和聲上以相同的步調共同推進，這是非常常見的織度。許多時候，一條旋律居於主導地位，其他聲部則用來充實和聲。主調織度有時進一步分為同節奏織度與旋律加伴奏兩個子類。

Homophony

A homophonic texture is characterized by having multiple voices moving together harmonically at the same pace. This is a very common texture. Many times, this takes the form of having a single melody that predominates, while other voices are used to fill out the harmonies. Homophony is sometimes further divided into two subcategories, homorhythm and melody and accompaniment.

同節奏織度

同節奏織度（homorhythm）是一種主調織度，所有聲部以極為相似或完全一致的節奏推進。它最常見於類似聖詠的作品中，旋律與和聲以柱式和弦一同移動。

請聆聽 Nikolai Tcherepnin 於 1910 年創作的《六首法國號四重奏》，第六首〈聖詠〉。注意旋律與和聲大多以柱式和弦一同移動，形成一致的節奏。

聆聽：六首樂曲，第六首〈聖詠〉（Spotify）

例 5。Nikolai Tcherepnin《六首法國號四重奏》，第六首〈聖詠〉，由 Deutsches Horn Ensemble 演奏。

接著聆聽 The Longest Johns 於 2018 年錄製的民謠〈Wild Mountain Thyme〉，片段為 0:20–0:44。記得，同節奏織度的各聲部具有相似的節奏。在此例中，雖有一條旋律從整體織度中突出，各聲部仍維持相同的節奏。

聆聽：Wild Mountain Thyme（Spotify）

例 6。The Longest Johns 的〈Wild Mountain Thyme〉；請聆聽 0:20–0:44。

Homorhythm

Homorhythm is a type of homophonic texture in which all voices move in an extremely similar or completely unison rhythm. This is most often seen in chorale-like compositions, where the melody and harmonies move together in block chords.

Let’s listen to Six Horn Quartets: no. 6, Chorale (1910), written by Nikolai Tcherepnin. Notice how both the melody and harmony move mostly in block chords, creating a unified rhythm.

Spotify Embed: Six Pieces: No. 6, Chorale

Example 5. Six Horn Quartets: no 6, Chorale by Nikolai Tcherepnin, performed by the Deutsches Horn Ensemble.

Now let’s listen to the folk song “Wild Mountain Thyme” recorded by The Longest Johns in 2018 (0:20–0:44). Remember that in a homorhythmic texture, there is a similarity of rhythm throughout all of the voices. In this example, there is a melody that stands out from the texture, but the voices still move in rhythmic unison.

Spotify Embed: Wild Mountain Thyme

Example 6. “Wild Mountain Thyme,” by The Longest Johns; listen from 0:20–0:44.

旋律與伴奏

旋律加伴奏也許是最常見的主調織度。其特徵是一條清楚的旋律，與其他支持聲部有所區別；這些支持聲部稱為伴奏。旋律與伴奏聲部通常使用不同節奏。

請聆聽保羅・興德米特《長笛奏鳴曲》（1936）的第二樂章。這個例子有十分清楚的旋律（長笛）與伴奏（鋼琴）。注意鋼琴從未完全與長笛採取相同節奏，但它藉由充實和聲織度，扮演伴奏角色。

聆聽：興德米特《長笛奏鳴曲》第二樂章〈非常慢〉（Sehr langsam）（Spotify）

例 7。保羅・興德米特《長笛奏鳴曲》，由 Emmanuel Pahud 與 Eric Le Sage 演奏。

接著聆聽 Erroll Garner 創作、Ella Fitzgerald 演唱的〈Misty〉（1954）。注意鋼琴如何為歌者 Fitzgerald 演唱的主要旋律伴奏。

聆聽：Misty（Spotify）

例 8。〈Misty〉（1954），Erroll Garner 創作，Ella Fitzgerald 演唱。

Melody and Accompaniment

A melody and accompaniment texture is perhaps the most common type of homophony. This texture is characterized by a clear melody that is distinct from other supporting voices, which are called an accompaniment. Often the melody will have a different rhythm from the supporting voice(s).

Let’s listen to the second movement of Paul Hindemith’s Flute Sonata (1936). This example features a very clear melody (flute) and accompaniment (piano). Notice how the piano is never completely in rhythmic unison with the flute; however, it provides the role of accompaniment by filling out the texture harmonically.

Spotify Embed: Hindemith: Flute Sonata: II. Sehr langsam

Example 7. Flute Sonata by Paul Hindemith, performed by Emmanuel Pahud and Eric Le Sage.

Now let’s listen to “Misty” (1954), written by Erroll Garner and performed by Ella Fitzgerald. Notice how the piano accompanies the primary melody sung by Fitzgerald (the vocalist).

Spotify Embed: Misty

Example 8. “Misty” (1954), written by Erroll Garner and performed by Ella Fitzgerald.

複調織度

複調織度的特徵是多個聲部各有不同的旋律線與節奏。換句話說，每個聲部都有獨立旋律，而這些獨立聲部融合在一起，形成和聲。

西方古典音樂的賦格經常呈現複調織度，例如德米特里・蕭士塔高維契創作的〈D 大調第五號賦格〉（1951–1952）。注意每條旋律線各自獨立，但一起聆聽時，各聲部又構成整體和聲。

聆聽：蕭士塔高維契〈D 大調第五號賦格〉（Spotify）

例 9。德米特里・蕭士塔高維契〈D 大調第五號賦格〉，Joachim Kwetzinsky 演奏。

強納森・拉森創作的百老匯音樂劇《吉屋出租（Rent）》（1996）中，〈I’ll Cover You – Reprise〉最後的副歌（2:20–2:45）也能聽到這種織度。注意其中三個獨立的人聲層次：它們唱著不同旋律與節奏，卻共同形成新的整體和聲：

聆聽：I’ll Cover You – Reprise（Spotify）

例 10。強納森・拉森創作的百老匯音樂劇《吉屋出租（Rent）》（1996）中的〈I’ll Cover You – Reprise〉；請聆聽 2:20–2:45。

多數音樂作品的織度都會有所變化。例如，你可能聽過先以獨唱或獨奏開始，再轉成旋律加伴奏的作品。織度的組合有許多不同可能！

Polyphony

Polyphony is characterized by multiple voices with separate melodic lines and rhythms. In other words, each voice has its own independent melodic line, and the independent voices blend together to create harmonies.

In Western classical music, polyphony is commonly heard in fugues, such as Fugue no. 5 in D Major (1951–1952), written by Dmitri Shostakovich. Notice how each individual melodic line is independent, yet the voices create harmonies overall when heard together.

Spotify Embed: Shostakovich: Fugue No. 5 In D Major

Example 9. Fugue no. 5 in D Major by Dmitri Shostakovich, performed by Joachim Kwetzinsky.

This can also be heard in the final chorus of “I’ll Cover You – Reprise” from the Broadway musical Rent (1996), written by Jonathan Larson (2:20–2:45). Notice how there are three independent vocal layers, singing different melodies and rhythms, but working together to create new harmonies overall:

Spotify Embed: I&apos;ll Cover You - Reprise

Example 10. “I’ll Cover You – Reprise” from the Broadway musical Rent (1996), written by Jonathan Larson; listen from 2:20–2:45.

Most musical works have some variety in texture. For example, you may have heard a work that opened with a solo voice or instrument, then changed to a melody with accompaniment. There are many different possibilities!
