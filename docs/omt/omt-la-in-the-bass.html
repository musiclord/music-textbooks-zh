低音 la（第六級）在樂句開頭、中段與結尾的用法

John Peterson

重點整理

本章討論作曲家如何依低音 la [latex](\hat6)[/latex] 在樂句中的位置——開頭、中段或結尾——採用不同的和聲配置。

  • 在樂句開頭，低音 la [latex](\hat6)[/latex] 常以兩種方式延長主和聲：
    • 以 IV6 配置和聲，形成 [latex]\mathrm{I-IV^6-V^6_5-I}[/latex] 進行（例 1 與例 3）。
    • 以變格用法的 (IV6) 配置和聲，形成 [latex]\mathrm{I-IV^6-I^6}[/latex] 進行（例 2 與例 4）。
  • 在樂句中段，低音 la [latex](\hat6)[/latex] 常用於：
    • 阻礙進行 V(7)–vi（例 5 與例 7）。
    • 以 vi 配置和聲，連接主和聲（T）與強屬前和聲（PD）區域（例 6 與例 8）。
  • 在樂句結尾，低音 la [latex](\hat6)[/latex] 常用於：
    • 形成弗里吉安半終止，即小調中的 iv6–V（例 7 與例 9）。
    • 代替預期中屬於強屬前和聲的低音 fa [latex](\hat4)[/latex]（例 10）。
譯註：原文重點整理的最後兩項譜例編號，與本章正文、圖說不一致：弗里吉安半終止實際對應例 9（樂曲摘錄）與例 10（寫作），不是所列的例 7 與例 9；以 la 代替強屬前低音的內容對應例 11，不是所列的例 10。正文保留來源編號供對照；此處依本章文字與圖說指出對應，尚未以待補的內嵌譜面核對。

Key Takeaways

This chapter discusses the various ways in which composers harmonize la [latex](\hat6)[/latex] in the bass depending on where it's found within a phrase: beginning, middle, or ending.

  • At phrase beginnings, la [latex](\hat6)[/latex] is often used to prolong tonic in two ways:
    • harmonized with IV6 in the progression [latex]\mathrm{I-IV^6-V^6_5-I}[/latex] (Examples 1 and 3)
    • harmonized with plagal (IV6) in the progression [latex]\mathrm{I-IV^6-I^6}[/latex] (Examples 2 and 4)
  • At phrase middles, la [latex](\hat6)[/latex] is often used:
    • in a deceptive motion (V(7)–vi) (Examples 5 and 7)
    • to connect the tonic (T) and strong predominant (PD) areas, harmonized with vi (Examples 6 and 8)
  • At phrase endings, la [latex](\hat6)[/latex] is often used:
    • to create a phrygian half cadence (iv6–V in minor) (Examples 7 and 9)
    • as a "stand-in" for the expected strong PD note fa [latex](\hat4)[/latex] (Example 10)

Overview: uses of la (scale degree 6)

So far, we've seen every scale degree appear in the bass except for la [latex](\hat6)[/latex]. In this chapter, we see that la [latex](\hat6)[/latex] is commonly harmonized by two chords: vi or IV6. It may show up in beginnings, middles, or endings of phrases, and each location is associated with particular progressions that involve la [latex](\hat6)[/latex] in the bass.

在樂句開頭

當 la [latex](\hat6)[/latex] 出現在樂句開頭的低音時，通常以兩種方式之一延長主和聲：（1）在 [latex]\mathrm{I-IV^6-V^6_5-I}[/latex] 進行中，以 IV6 作為屬前和弦（例 1）；或（2）在 [latex]\mathrm{I-IV^6-I^6}[/latex] 進行中，使用變格用法的 (IV6)（例 2）。

At phrase beginnings

When la [latex](\hat6)[/latex] appears in the bass at the beginning of a phrase, it typically prolongs tonic in one of two ways: (1) using IV6 as a predominant in the progression [latex]\mathrm{I-IV^6-V^6_5-I}[/latex] (Example 1), or (2) using plagal (IV6) in the progression [latex]\mathrm{I-IV^6-I^6}[/latex] (Example 2).

以 IV6 作為屬前和弦

[latex]\mathrm{I-IV^6-V^6_5-I}[/latex] 的寫作可能不太容易，尤其是 [latex]\mathrm{IV^6}[/latex] 接到 [latex]\mathrm{V^6}[/latex] 時，既有平行音程的風險，也可能重複導音。沒有任何單一建議能保證避開所有問題。寫作 [latex]\mathrm{I-IV^6-V^6_5-I}[/latex] 時，女高音採用 do–do–re–do [latex](\hat1-\hat1-\hat2-\hat1)[/latex] 或 mi–mi–fa–mi [latex](\hat3-\hat3-\hat4-\hat3)[/latex] 會有幫助，但仍須仔細檢查平行。以 [latex]\mathrm{V^6_5}[/latex] 代替 [latex]\mathrm{V^6}[/latex]，也能降低部分平行風險（例 3）。請注意，這個進行在小調中不太適用，因為低音從 le 到 ti [latex](\downarrow\hat6-\uparrow\hat7)[/latex] 會形成增二度。小調中少數使用此進行的情況，會將 le 升為 la，由 [latex](\downarrow\hat6[/latex] 改成 [latex]\uparrow\hat6)[/latex]，以避免增二度（例 3c 與例 3d）。

Using IV6 as a predominant

Writing [latex]\mathrm{I-IV^6-V^6_5-I}[/latex] can be tricky, particularly when [latex]\mathrm{IV^6}[/latex] goes to [latex]\mathrm{V^6}[/latex], since there is a danger both of parallels and of a doubled leading tone. No single piece of advice can guarantee that you’ll avoid problems. When you write [latex]\mathrm{I-IV^6-V^6_5-I}[/latex], the soprano lines do–do–re–do [latex](\hat1-\hat1-\hat2-\hat1)[/latex] and mi–mi–fa–mi [latex](\hat3-\hat3-\hat4-\hat3)[/latex] will help, but you'll need to check carefully for parallels. Using [latex]\mathrm{V^6_5}[/latex] instead of [latex]\mathrm{V^6}[/latex] will also mitigate some of the danger of parallels (Example 3). Note that this progression doesn't work well in minor, where the bass would create an augmented second from le to ti [latex](\downarrow\hat6-\uparrow\hat7)[/latex]. The rare occurrences of this progression in minor raise le to la [latex](\downarrow\hat6[/latex] to [latex]\uparrow\hat6)[/latex] to avoid the augmented second (Examples 3c and 3d).

Using plagal (IV6)

Writing [latex]\mathrm{I-IV^6-I^6}[/latex] is relatively easy (Example 4). Here, remember three things:

  1. The bass always arpeggiates down.
  2. The most common soprano is mi–fa–sol [latex](\hat3-\hat4-\hat5)[/latex].
  3. The other voices should move by step or common tone.

At phrase middles

In the middle of a phrase, la [latex](\hat6)[/latex] shows up in the bass in one of two ways:

  1. To avoid a cadence as part of a deceptive motion (Example 5)

阻礙進行

當 V 和弦讓人期待終止式，卻轉向出乎預期的非主和聲時，就稱為阻礙進行（deceptive motion）。最常見的情況是 V(7) 接到 vi 而非 I，低音由 sol 走到 la [latex](\hat5-\hat6)[/latex]。較少見的情況，則是在阻礙進行中，為 la [latex](\hat6)[/latex] 配置 IV6 而非 vi。V(7)–vi 的阻礙進行本身就有平行風險；以下兩點可以避免這些問題（例 7）：（1）照常將 ti 解決到 do [latex](\hat7-\hat1)[/latex]；（2）讓所有上方聲部都與低音反向進行，也就是向下行。

譯註：原文第二點寫「所有上方聲部」向下，與第一點 ti–do 上行解決互相矛盾。依這兩項指示的關係，第二點應理解為其餘上方聲部向下，不能把已由 ti 上行到 do 的導音聲部也改成下行。英文原句及直譯保留，這是對文字矛盾的釐清；待補譜例尚未目視核對。

阻礙進行與阻礙終止

有些人用「阻礙終止」一詞，描述我們所稱的「阻礙進行」。由於 V(7)–vi 是避開終止式，而非形成終止式，我們認為「阻礙終止」無法準確描述這個進行的作用，因此偏好較中性的「阻礙進行」。

Deceptive motion

When a V chord sets up the expectation for a cadence, but moves instead to an unexpected, non-tonic harmony, this is called deceptive motion. Deceptive motion most commonly occurs when V(7) moves to vi rather than I, with the bass moving sol–la [latex](\hat5-\hat6)[/latex]. Less commonly, la [latex](\hat6)[/latex] may be harmonized with IV6 rather than vi in a deceptive motion. Writing deceptive motion with V(7)–vi carries an inherent danger of parallels that can be avoided by doing the following two things (Example 7): (1) resolve ti–do [latex](\hat7-\hat1)[/latex] as you would do normally, and (2) move all upper voices in contrary motion to the bass (downward).

Example 7. Writing deceptive motion.

Deceptive motion vs. Deceptive cadence

Some people use the term “deceptive cadence” to describe what we refer to as “deceptive motion.” Since the progression V(7)–vi avoids a cadence rather than creating one, we find that the term “deceptive cadence” inaccurately describes the progression’s purpose, so we prefer the more neutral description “deceptive motion.”

以 vi 作為弱屬前和弦

弗里吉安半終止（PHC）

弗里吉安半終止（PHC）是一種特殊的樂句終止方式，只出現在小調，使用 iv6–V 進行。之所以稱為「弗里吉安」，是因為低音 le 走到 sol [latex](\downarrow\hat6-\hat5)[/latex] 時形成半音，其音響類似弗里吉安調式中的 ra 走到 do [latex](\downarrow\hat2-\hat1)[/latex]。這個進行有平行音程的風險，也可能在 le 與 ti [latex](\downarrow\hat6-\uparrow\hat7)[/latex] 之間寫出增二度。選擇在 iv6 中重複 do [latex](\hat1)[/latex]，便能避免這些問題（例 10）。弗里吉安半終止很常從 i 接入，女高音則採用 me–fa–sol [latex](\hat3-\hat4-\hat5)[/latex]，如例 10 所示。

The phrygian half cadence (PHC)

The phrygian half cadence (PHC) is a special kind of cadential phrase ending that occurs only in minor and involves the progression iv6–V. It's called “phrygian” because of the half step that occurs when le moves to sol [latex](\downarrow\hat6-\hat5)[/latex] in the bass, a sound that’s similar to when ra moves to do [latex](\downarrow\hat2-\hat1)[/latex] in the phrygian mode. The progression carries a danger of parallels and of writing an augmented second between le and ti [latex](\downarrow\hat6-\uparrow\hat7)[/latex]. These can be avoided if you choose to double do [latex](\hat1)[/latex] in the iv6 chord (Example 10). Very often, PHCs are approached from i, and are accompanied by the soprano me–fa–sol [latex](\hat3-\hat4-\hat5)[/latex], as in Example 10.
