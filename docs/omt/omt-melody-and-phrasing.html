旋律與樂句

Bryn Hughes and Megan Lavengood

重點整理

流行／搖滾歌曲的段落，通常包含兩、三或四個樂句，常見組織方式如下：

  • 兩部分：aa′。
  • 三部分：aa′b，常見於十二小節藍調。
  • 四部分：srdc，即陳述、重述／回應、轉離、結束。

本章討論主歌、副歌、橋段等歌曲段落的內部結構。每個段落至少包含兩個樂句；在流行／搖滾音樂中，一個樂句通常約四小節，對應一行歌詞。分析這些結構時，以小寫字母標示樂句。

譯註：這是本章對流行／搖滾段落的基本概括。下一章補充：有些輔助段落可以只有一個樂句。

Key Takeaways

Sections in pop/rock music typically consist of two, three, or four phrases. These phrases are usually organized as follows:

  • Two-part: aa′
  • Three-part: aa′b (often a 12-bar blues)
  • Four-part: srdc (statement, restatement/response, departure, conclusion)

Chapter Playlist

This chapter discusses the structure of song sections such as a verse, chorus, or bridge. Each section consists of at least two phrases—in pop/rock music, a phrase is a musical unit that typically lasts for four bars and corresponds to one line of the lyrics. In labeling these structures, phrases are designated by lowercase letters.

兩部分的樂句組織

若段落中的樂句能分成前、後兩半，就是兩部分結構。後半通常以與前半相同的音樂為基礎，因此標作aa′。兩半常以相同材料開始，卻有不同結尾，形成前句—後句的關係，也就是由較弱的收束走向較強的收束。

譯註：這裡的兩、三、四部分是段落內的樂句組織，不等同整首曲子的二段式、三段式。前句→後句的弱→強，指終止或收束力度，不是音量。

〈Livin’ on a Prayer〉副歌（1:33）採aa′結構（例1）。第一個四小節樂句從「喔，我們已走了一半……」開始，第二個從「握住我的手……」開始。兩句旋律與和聲相同，所以都用a；歌詞不同，因此第二句加上撇號，標成a′，讀作「a prime」。很多歌曲未必如此界線分明，但只要兩個樂句開頭使用相似材料，就可用相同字母；新歌詞、不同結尾或音樂變化，則以撇號表示。

很少數段落的前、後兩半，會使用明顯不同的音樂；這時標作ab。

歌詞樂句
喔，我們已走了一半
喔，靠著祈禱過日子		a
握住我的手，我發誓我們一定撐得過去
喔，靠著祈禱過日子		a′

例1。〈Livin’ on a Prayer〉。

Two-Part

A section is two-part when the phrases that make up the section can be grouped into a first half and a second half. In two-part sections, the second half is usually based on the same music as the first half, and thus it is labeled aa′. Often these two halves begin the same but have different endings, participating in an antecedent–consequent (weak → strong) relationship.

The chorus to “Livin’ on a Prayer” (1'33") has an aa′ structure (Example 1). The first four-bar phrase (“Oh, we’re half-way there…”) and the second four-bar phrase (“Take my hand…”) have identical melody and harmony (so they both get the letter a), but different lyrics (so the second a is marked as "a prime": a′). Note that in many songs, this relationship is not as clear cut. However, if the two phrases begin with similar musical material, give them the same letter. New lyrics, new musical endings, or musical variations simply warrant a “prime.”

Very rarely, a section's phrases can be grouped into two clear halves based on different music. Such a section is labeled ab.

Lyrics Phrase
Whoa, we're halfway there
Whoa, livin' on a prayer		 a
Take my hand, we'll make it I swear
Whoa, livin' on a prayer.		 a′

Example 1. "Livin' on a Prayer."

三部分的樂句組織

包含三個樂句的段落，構成三部分結構。如果前兩句以相同音樂為基礎，第三句不同，就標作aa′b。

十二小節藍調進行是aa′b三部分段落最常見的例子。〈Hound Dog〉的歌節，就是aa′b（例2）。

歌詞樂句
你不過是條獵犬
整天哀叫		a
你不過是條獵犬
整天哀叫		a′
你從沒抓到兔子，也不是我的朋友。b
譯註：兩個a句的歌詞相同，仍可因音樂上的變化標成a′。撇號不只表示換歌詞；本章以音樂材料為主要分類依據。

例2。〈Hound Dog〉。

Three-Part

A section containing three phrases is a three-part section. If the first two phrases are based on the same music and the third is different, the section is labeled aa′b.

12-bar blues progressions are the most common example of a three-part aa′b section. “Hound Dog”  contains aa′b strophes (Example 2).

Lyrics Phrase
You ain't nothin' but a hound dog
Cryin' all the time 		a
You ain't nothin' but a hound dog
Cryin' all the time 		a′
Well, you ain't never caught a rabbit and you ain't no friend of mine. b

Example 2. "Hound Dog."

四部分的樂句組織

四個樂句組成的段落，常有類似樂句型（sentence）的功能進程：呈示→延續→終止／結束。在流行／搖滾中，第一句提出基本樂思，第二句重複或回應，第三句使用對比材料，常伴隨片段化、和聲節奏加快及離開主和聲；第四句則作結。結尾可以返回基本樂思與主和聲，也可以使用新材料，在旋律、節奏與和聲上形成有力收束。Walter Everett（2001，132頁）把這種四樂句的組織稱為srdc：陳述（statement）、重述／回應（restatement/response）、轉離（departure）、結束（conclusion）。

譯註：這是把古典樂句型的呈示、延續與終止功能，類比到較大的四樂句段落，不把phrase與sentence一律混成同一個詞。

若按一般材料字母標記，srdc可能是aaba：陳述材料在重述與結尾再現；也可能是aabc，表示結尾使用新材料。偶爾也能是abcd或abca，但前提是b明確回應a，不能只是毫無關聯的新材料。

srdc通常能清楚分成兩半：sr與dc。

Bobby Darin的〈Dream Lover〉（例3），就是四部分srdc樂句組織的典型例子。

歌詞樂句
每晚我都盼望、祈禱
夢中情人會走入我的生命		s（陳述）
一個能擁在懷裡的女孩
讓我體會她魅力的魔法		r（重複）
因為我想要（耶—耶，耶）
一個女孩（耶—耶，耶）
能稱作（耶—耶，耶）
屬於我（耶—耶）		d（轉離）
我想要一個夢中情人
好讓我不必獨自做夢		c（結束）

例3。〈Dream Lover〉。

Four-Part

A section composed of four phrases often contains a sentential structure (presentation → continuation → cadential/conclusion). In pop/rock music, this often appears as a basic musical idea in the first phrase, a repetition or “response” to it in the second, contrasting material in the third phrase (often employing fragmentation, acceleration of harmonic rhythm, and movement away from tonic harmony), and a conclusion in the fourth phrase―either with a return to the basic idea and tonic harmony or with still newer material that forms a strong melodic, rhythmic, and harmonic conclusion. Walter Everett (2001, 132) has called such a four-phrase sentential structure in pop/rock music srdc (statement, restatement/response, departure, conclusion).

In conventional lettering, an srdc section could employ an aaba structure (with statement material returning as a restatement and again as the conclusion), or an aabc structure (where the conclusion material is new). Occasionally abcd or abca are possible, but only if b is a clear response to a, not simply new material.

An srdc structure tends to tend to divide neatly into halves: sr and dc.

Bobby Darin's "Dream Lover" (Example 3) provides a classic example of a four-part srdc phrase structure.

Lyrics Phrase
Every night I hope and pray
A dream lover will come my way		 s (statement)
A girl to hold in my arms
And know the magic of her charms		 r (repetition)
'Cause I want (yeah-yeah, yeah)
A girl (yeah-yeah, yeah)
To call (yeah-yeah, yeah)
My own (yeah-yeah)		 d (departure)
I want a dream lover
So I don't have to dream alone		 c (conclusion)

Example 3. "Dream Lover."
