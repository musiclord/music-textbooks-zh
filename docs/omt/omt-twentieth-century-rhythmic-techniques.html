二十世紀節奏技法

Samuel Brady and Chelsey Hamm

重點整理

  • 不對稱拍子將小節內的拍或分割單位，組成長短不等的群組，形成不均等的拍節脈動。
  • 無拍節音樂可能有拍號，也可能沒有；無論如何，都感受不到明確拍節。
  • 樂譜寫出的拍子，有時不同於聽者感受到的拍子；這涉及感知拍節與記譜拍節的差異。
  • 變換拍子是在作品中改變拍子的作曲手法。
  • 兩種以上拍子同時進行，就形成多重拍子。
  • 節奏轉調在原速度中引入某種細分或拍的組合，使其時值對應新速度中的音值，藉此讓原本突兀的速度轉換變得平順。
  • 時間軸記譜以秒數衡量時間，取代傳統小節線與拍節。
  • 扇形符槓表示同一組符槓內的音逐漸加快或減慢。
  • 固定音型是反覆出現的節奏或音高材料。

二十世紀的許多作曲家，擴充了西方藝術音樂原有的節奏語彙。以下討論九種較不傳統的節奏技法。它們未必是「新」發明：其中許多早已存在於非西方音樂，甚至1900年以前的西方音樂中；只是1900年之後，西方作曲家更常採用。

Key Takeaways

  • Asymmetrical meters contain measures which are divided into unequal groupings of beats or divisions, creating an uneven metrical pulse.
  • Ametric music may or may not have an explicit meter signature, and does not have any perceivable meter.
  • In some cases, the meter written in the score may not be the meter that the listener perceives, which is an issue of perceived versus notated meter.
  • Changing meter is a method of composition which incorporates any change of meter.
  • Polymeter occurs when two or more meters are performed simultaneously.
  • Metric modulation is a rhythmic technique that smooths out abrupt tempo changes by introducing subdivisions or groups of beats in the first tempo that match durations in the new tempo.
  • Timeline notation is a contemporary metric technique that uses seconds as the measure of time, rather than traditional bar lines and meters.
  • Feathered beaming is a gradual change in the speed of notes within a single beam.
  • Ostinato is a repeated rhythmic or pitched musical idea.

Many composers in the twentieth century expanded the traditional rhythmic palette of Western art music. Below, nine less traditional rhythmic techniques are discussed. These rhythmic techniques were not necessarily "new;" many of them were found in non-Western music or even Western music before 1900. However, Western composers did incorporate them more frequently after this year.

Asymmetrical Meter

Music in asymmetrical meters contain measures which are divided into unequal groupings of beats or divisions, creating an uneven metrical pulse. It is important to note that the term "asymmetrical meter" is not universally adopted, and such meters are known by other names, such as "mixed meters," "complex meters," and "non-isochronous meters," among others. 無拍節音樂

以無拍節方式寫成的音樂，沒有可明確感知的拍節。無拍節音樂可能寫拍號，也可能不寫，演奏或演唱也不必採嚴格規律的拍節。換句話說，音符仍可有節奏時值，但這些時值不一定形成能感知的拍型。這種手法可讓演奏者自由即興與處理樂句，出現在某些裝飾奏、自由即興段落、葛利果聖歌及後調性音樂中。請聽例5這首沒有拍號的後調性歌曲：

譯註：無拍節不等於沒有節奏或所有時值都任意。例5原譜明確要求均勻、機械式演奏，整段速度與音量不變；這與未形成規律拍節並不矛盾。

Ametric Music

Music written ametrically does not have any perceivable meter. Ametric music may or may not have an explicit meter signature, and is not necessarily played or sung in a strict metrical style. In other words, the notes may still have rhythmic values, but rhythmic durations might not result in a perceivable beat pattern. This technique may allow for a performer’s own improvisation and phrasing, and is employed in some cadenzas, passages of free improvisation, Gregorian chant, and post-tonal music. Timeline Notation

Timeline notation is a contemporary metric technique that uses seconds as the measure of time, rather than traditional bar lines and meters. Composers indicate groups of seconds in uneven groupings or an even grid. This technique often results in a feeling of ameter. Timeline notation can also be accompanied by graphic notation, in which pitch and durations are specified by nonstandard symbols, as seen in Example 15:

Penderecki - Threnody (Animated Score)

Example 15. Timeline notation in Krzysztof Penderecki's "Threnody for the Victims of Hiroshima" (1960).

Threnody for the Victims of Hiroshima (1960) was written by Krzystof Penderecki. It was originally called 8'37"; later, it was renamed to Threnody, and was eventually dedicated to the victims of the bombing of Hiroshima in 1964. This composition features timeline notation, graphic notation, and extended string techniques to create a jarring chaos that evokes programmatic sounds such as sirens or screams.

Notice how the number of seconds is split unevenly on this timeline. First there is a section that is 10 seconds in length, then one that is 7 seconds, followed by two more that are 10 seconds and 5 seconds respectively.

Water Walk for Solo Television Performer (1959) is a composition written by John Cage that was premiered on the television program "Lascia o Raddoppia" in Milan in 1959. The composition calls for 34 different objects, as well as a prerecorded single track tape. Most of the materials relate to water in some way; some objects include a bath tub, rubber duck, ice cubes, etc. Additionally, Cage also calls for five radios and a grand piano.

扇形符槓記錄同一組符槓內的音逐漸改變速度。比較結尾與開頭所暗示的音值：結尾音值較長，表示減速；較短，則表示加速。例19示範這種記法：

例19第一行表示加速，第二行表示減速。這被視為一種擴展技法，最常見於當代作品，有時也出現在裝飾奏及無拍節作品。由於加速或減速的過程不容易預先按規律拍節掌握，因此較少用於拍節十分嚴格的作品。

Feathered Notes

Feathered beaming notates a gradual change in the speed of notes within a single beam. One can distinguish deceleration from acceleration based upon whether the value of the beam's final note is longer or shorter than the note that the beam began with. Feathered notes are shown in Example 19:

Example 19. Feathered notes.

In Example 19, the first line shows an acceleration while the second line shows a deceleration. Feathered notes are considered to be an extended technique, most often heard in contemporary compositions, sometimes including cadenzas and ametric works. Due to the unpredictable nature of the acceleration or deceleration of the rhythm, feathered notes are not usually found in strictly metered compositions.
