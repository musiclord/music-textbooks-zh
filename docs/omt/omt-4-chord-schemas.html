四和弦圖式

Bryn Hughes and Megan Lavengood

重點整理

I、IV、V與vi是流行音樂最常見的和聲，可以排列成幾種聲響各異的圖式。每種圖式都能透過和弦替代或輪換起點等方式變化，仍讓人辨認出原圖式。

  • Doo-wop圖式為I–vi–IV–V，常見於1960年代流行音樂。常見變化：
    • I–vi–ii–V：以ii取代IV。
    • IV–V–I–vi：輪換起點。
  • 創作歌手圖式為vi–IV–I–V或I–V–vi–IV，常見於1990年代創作歌手的音樂。也能以關係小調理解為i–VI–III–VII。常見變化是輪換為IV–I–V–vi。
  • 跳格子圖式為IV–V–vi–I，常見於2010年以來的流行音樂。也能以關係小調理解為VI–VII–i–III。常見變化為VI–V–i–III，也就是在小調中以V取代VII。

本章播放清單

本章討論的進行，都有一個共同點：使用I、IV、V與vi這四個和弦，它們可能是整個流行音樂中最常用的和弦。因此，這些圖式聽起來有些相似；差別在和弦排列順序。

Key Takeaways

I, IV, V, and vi are the most common harmonies in pop music, and they can be arranged into several schemas, each with a distinct sound. Each schema can have variations, such as chord substitution or rotation, while still remaining recognizable as that schema.

  • The doo-wop schema is I–vi–IV–V, and it was common in 1960s pop music. Common variations:
    • I–vi–ii–V (ii substitutes for IV)
    • IV–V–I–vi (rotation)
  • The singer/songwriter schema is vi–IV–I–V or I–V–vi–IV, and it was common in 1990s singer/songwriter music. It can also be understood in its relative minor: i–VI–III–VII. A common variation is IV–I–V–vi (rotation).
  • The hopscotch schema is IV–V–vi–I, and it is common in recent pop music (since 2010). It can also be understood in its relative minor: VI–VII–i–III. A common variation is VI–V–i–III (V substitutes for VII in minor).

Chapter Playlist

The progressions discussed in this chapter all have something in common. They use the same four chords: I, IV, V, and vi, which are probably the most common chords in all of pop music. Because of this, they all sound somewhat similar; the difference is in the order of the chords.

以ii取代IV

由於ii與IV在這個和弦進行中具有相同功能，兩者可以互相替代，例如Bruce Springsteen〈Hungry Heart〉（1980）。

Substituting ii for IV

Because ii and IV share the same function in this chord progression, ii can be swapped out for IV, as in Bruce Springsteen's "Hungry Heart" (1980).

輪換起點

Doo-wop圖式通常循環使用，因此也能由循環中的另一個和弦開始，仍按原來的順序前進，稱為輪換起點。例如Coldplay〈Viva la Vida〉（2008）反覆使用同一串和弦，但樂句由IV而非I開始（例2）。

Rotation

Because the doo-wop schema is typically employed in cycles, it can also start on a different chord in the cycle and then proceed through the same succession of chords (rotation). For example, “Viva la Vida” by Coldplay (2008) works through a cyclical repetition of the same succession of chords, but the phrases begin on IV rather than I (Example 2).

Singer/Songwriter

The singer/songwriter schema may be the best known of all the four-chord schemas. A common Roman numeral analysis for this schema is vi–IV–I–V, or Ami–F–C–G in C major, but the rotation starting with I is also exceedingly common. On top of that, either rotation may be understood as having either the major tonic or the relative minor tonic. This is best understood through Example 3.

調性模糊

這個進行的重要特徵，是單靠它本身，無法清楚確立唯一的主和弦，稱為調性模糊。例如Luis Fonsi與Daddy Yankee的〈Despacito〉（2017），和弦進行Bmi–G–D–A，對不同聽者而言，可能是D大調的vi–IV–I–V，也可能是B小調的i–VI–III–VII（例4）。創作歌手圖式之所以模糊，部分原因是沒有正格終止：兩種可能的收束進行，分別是變格IV–I或級進VII–i。缺乏強烈的和聲終止時，聽者可能只能依前後的和弦進行、循環由哪些和弦開始與結束，以及旋律中的重要音，判斷主和弦；也可能始終無法確定。

Tonal ambiguity

One important feature of this progression is that it does not, on its own, clearly communicate a definitive tonic chord. This property is known as tonal ambiguity. An example is "Despacito" by Luis Fonsi and Daddy Yankee (2017). The chord progression, Bmi–G–D–A, can sound like vi–IV–I–V in D major or like i–VI–III–VII in B minor to different listeners (Example 4). One reason the singer/songwriter schema is ambiguous is because there is no authentic cadence: the two potential cadential motions are either plagal (IV–I) or stepwise (VII–i). Without a strong harmonic cadence, listeners might only be able to determine the tonic chord—if at all—by the progressions before and after the singer/songwriter schema, which chords in the cycle begin and end it, and the important pitches of the melody.

輪換起點

如前文所述，這個圖式從I或vi開始的兩種輪換，同樣常見。有時也從IV開始，形成IV–I–V–vi，以V–vi的假終止作結，因而成為帶有假解決的變體。Lady Gaga〈Alejandro〉（2009）的副歌，從1:11開始，就使用這種輪換。

Rotations

As discussed above, this schema has two equally common rotations that start the progression on I and vi. From time to time, the singer/songwriter progression might also begin on the IV chord, resulting in a “deceptive” variant of this progression that ends with V–vi—a deceptive cadence (IV–I–V–vi). The chorus (starts at 1:11) of "Alejandro" by Lady Gaga (2009) uses this rotation of the singer/songwriter schema.

跳格子圖式

約2010年以來，另一種四和弦圖式越來越常見：IV–V–vi–I，在小調中則為VI–VII–i–III。例子包括Sam Smith〈Dancing with a Stranger〉（2019），以及DJ Khaled、Justin Bieber與Quavo的〈No Brainer〉（2018）。我們稱它為跳格子圖式，因為它的根音進行是級進、級進、三度小跳。

譯註：連結的動圖是跳格子遊戲示意：黃色方格、紅色腳印與黑色標記，沒有和弦或音高標註。它說明圖式名稱的比喻，不是音樂譜例。

和創作歌手圖式一樣，跳格子圖式也可能具有調性模糊。換句話說，在F–G–Ami–C中，A小和弦或C大和弦，都可能被聽成主和弦（例6）。這個進行通常缺乏明確的終止動作，尤其是進入C和弦的時候。

Hopscotch

In recent years (since about 2010), another type of four-chord schema has become increasingly common: IV–V–vi–I, or VI–VII–i–III in minor.  Examples include "Dancing with a Stranger" by Sam Smith (2019) and "No Brainer" by DJ Khaled, Justin Bieber, and Quavo (2018). We will refer to this as the hopscotch schema because of its root motion: step, step, skip.

Like the singer/songwriter schema, the hopscotch schema can be tonally ambiguous. In other words, in the progression F–G–Ami–C, either Ami or C might sound like tonic (Example 6). There is often no definitive cadential motion, especially moving into the C chord.

在小調中以V取代VII

一種特別常見的和弦替代，可以強化跳格子圖式的小調理解：用大和弦V取代下主音和弦VII，讓VI–VII–i–III變成VI–V–i–III。BLACKPINK〈Love to Hate Me〉（2020）就這樣做。兩個和弦雖有相同的和聲功能，色彩卻相差很大：小調中的大V使用ti [latex](\hat7)[/latex]，VII則使用te [latex](\downarrow\hat7)[/latex]。有些歌曲，例如YNW Melly〈Mixed Personalities〉，將V轉位，讓低音仍保持跳格子的「級進—級進—三度小跳」，即使根音進行已經改變（例7）。

譯註：本段的「功能相同」採流行調式分析觀點：兩者都可導向小調主和弦。不能因此認為VII與V有相同音程結構或導音；V中的升高第七級，正是本段要比較的色彩差異。

Replacing VII in minor with V

An especially common harmonic substitution that encourages a minor-mode interpretation of the hopscotch schema is to replace the subtonic VII chord with the major V chord, so that VI–VII–i–III becomes VI–V–i–III. One song that does this is "Love to Hate Me" by BLACKPINK (2020). Although these chords have the same harmonic function, the two chords have quite distinct colors, since the major V chord in minor uses ti [latex](\hat7)[/latex] while VII uses te [latex](\downarrow\hat7)[/latex]. Some songs, like "Mixed Personalities" by YNW Melly, invert this V chord, which allows the bass motion of the hopscotch schema to stay the same (step-step-skip) even though the root motion has changed (Example 7).

用耳朵辨認

這些四和弦圖式都使用I、IV、V與vi，因此聽起來相似。而且每種圖式都可以輪換，不能只看進行由哪個和弦開始、在哪個和弦結束！請改聽大調主和弦如何被接近（例8）。

三種圖式接近大調主和弦的方式

靜態樂譜尚待補齊；原資源包含本章使用的譜例。


例8。每種圖式都從不同和弦接入大調主和弦。
  • Doo-wop圖式以十分傳統的正格進行接入主和弦，和古典音樂相似。
  • 創作歌手圖式以變格進行接入大調主和弦。
  • 跳格子圖式以三度小跳接入大調主和弦，與傳統終止類型沒有直接對應關係。
譯註：這裡辨認的是接入和弦的進行方向，不表示循環中每次V–I或IV–I都已構成樂句結尾的完整終止；正格進行的詞條連到本書正格終止定義，可對照其和聲關係。

即使你認為真正的主和弦是小調主和弦，聽大調主和弦如何被接近，仍有助於區分這幾種四和弦圖式。只聽小調主和弦的接近方式，可能沒有那麼有用，因為創作歌手與跳格子圖式，都是以級進接入小調主和弦。

Recognizing by Ear

All of these four-chord schemas sound similar to one another, since they all use I, IV, V, and vi. All the schemas can be rotated, so it's not simply a matter of seeing where the progression begins and ends! Instead, try listening to how the major tonic is approached (Example 8).

Musical example

Static score pending; the original resource contains a musical example.


Example 8. Each schema approaches the major tonic from a different chord.
  • In the doo-wop schema, the tonic is approached with very traditional authentic motion, as in classical music.
  • In the singer/songwriter schema, the major tonic is approached with plagal motion.
  • In the hopscotch schema, the major tonic is approached by a skip (not related to any traditional cadence).

Even if you think the real tonic is the minor tonic, listening to the approach to the major tonic will help distinguish among these four-chord schema options. Listening to the approach to the minor tonic may not be helpful, since both the singer/songwriter and the hopscotch schemas approach the minor tonic by step.
