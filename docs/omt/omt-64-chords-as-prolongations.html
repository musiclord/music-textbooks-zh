以四六和弦延長和聲

John Peterson

重點整理

本章介紹終止 [latex]^6_4[/latex] 以外的另外三種 [latex]^6_4[/latex] 和弦。

  • 經過（passing，簡寫 pass.）[latex]^6_4[/latex]：以 [latex]^6_4[/latex] 和弦為低音中的經過音配置和聲。通常用於延長主和聲或屬前和聲，且總是位於兩個功能相同的和弦之間。
  • 鄰音（neighbor，簡寫 n.）[latex]^6_4[/latex]：低音保持不動，上方兩個聲部作上鄰音進行。通常用於延長主和聲或屬和聲，前後兩個和弦一定都是原位。
  • 琶音（arpeggiating，簡寫 arp.）[latex]^6_4[/latex]：低音以琶音進行經過和弦五音，上方聲部則以某種方式維持該和弦。它可以延長任何和聲，分析時通常不必特別標示。

下方例 6 的表格，彙整我們建議在分析中標示的三種 [latex]^6_4[/latex] 的特徵。

本章播放清單

目前我們已看到，主和聲（T）區域最常以屬功能和弦延長，尤其常用轉位的 [latex]\mathrm{V^7}[/latex]。本章將介紹另外幾種較少見的方法，不只可以延長主和弦，也能延長屬和弦與屬前和弦。前面說過，[latex]^6_4[/latex] 和弦含有與低音不協和的四度，因此需要特殊處理。我們已學過終止四六 [latex]\mathrm{cad.^6_4}[/latex]；這裡轉而討論 [latex]^6_4[/latex] 和弦的另外三種用法：經過 [latex]^6_4[/latex]、鄰音 [latex]^6_4[/latex] 與琶音 [latex]^6_4[/latex]。分析時，每遇到 [latex]^6_4[/latex] 和弦，都應先停下來辨認它的類型（終止、經過、鄰音或琶音），因為 [latex]^6_4[/latex] 的類型決定了它的標記。對於 [latex]^6_4[/latex] 和弦，只標羅馬數字並不足夠，還必須標明類型。

Key Takeaways

This chapter introduces three additional [latex]^6_4[/latex] chords beyond cadential [latex]^6_4[/latex].

  • Passing (pass.) [latex]^6_4[/latex] involves a passing tone in the bass that has been harmonized by a [latex]^6_4[/latex] chord. It typically prolongs tonic or predominant harmonies, and it always occurs between two chords of the same function.
  • Neighbor (n.) [latex]^6_4[/latex] involves a static bass above which two of the upper voices perform upper neighbor motion. It typically prolongs tonic or dominant harmonies, and the chords on both sides of it are always in root position.
  • Arpeggiating (arp.) [latex]^6_4[/latex] involves a bass that arpeggiates through the fifth of the chord while the upper voices sustain the chord in some way. It may prolong any harmony, and we don't typically bother recognizing it in analysis.

The table in Example 6 below summarizes the characteristics of each of the three types of [latex]^6_4[/latex] that we advocate labeling in analysis.

Chapter Playlist

So far, we've seen that the tonic (T) area is most commonly prolonged using dominant-function chords, especially inverted [latex]\mathrm{V^7}[/latex]s. In this chapter, we look at some additional, less common ways to prolong not only tonic chords, but also dominant and predominant chords. Earlier, we saw how [latex]^6_4[/latex] chords are treated in special ways because they contain a dissonance with the bass (the fourth). We've already learned about [latex]\mathrm{cad.^6_4}[/latex]; here, we turn to the three other ways [latex]^6_4[/latex] chords can be used: passing [latex]^6_4[/latex], neighboring [latex]^6_4[/latex], and arpeggiating [latex]^6_4[/latex]. Note that in analysis, whenever you encounter a [latex]^6_4[/latex] chord, you should stop and identify which kind it is (cadential, passing, neighboring, or arpeggiating) because the kind of [latex]^6_4[/latex] determines the label. For [latex]^6_4[/latex] chords, the Roman numeral by itself isn’t a sufficient label—the type also needs to be included.

經過 [latex]^6_4[/latex]

經過（pass.）[latex]^6_4[/latex] 是建立在低音經過音上的 [latex]^6_4[/latex] 和弦（例 1），最常用於延長主和聲或屬前和聲。請留意，[latex]\mathrm{pass.^6_4}[/latex] 前後的和弦，功能一定相同（例如 [latex]\mathrm{IV^6-pass.^6_4-ii^6}[/latex]），不能是不同功能（例如 [latex]\mathrm{IV^6-pass.^6_4-I}[/latex]）。

Passing [latex]^6_4[/latex]

The passing (pass.) [latex]^6_4[/latex] is a [latex]^6_4[/latex] chord built on a passing tone in the bass (Example 1). It's most commonly found prolonging tonic or predominant harmonies. Importantly, the chords on both sides of the [latex]\mathrm{pass.^6_4}[/latex] are always the same function (e.g., [latex]\mathrm{IV^6-pass.^6_4-ii^6}[/latex]), not of different function (e.g., [latex]\mathrm{IV^6-pass.^6_4-I}[/latex]).

Example 1. [latex]\mathrm{pass.^6_4}[/latex] in Beethoven, Piano Sonata Op. 14, no. 1, I, mm. 50–57 (1:28-1:42).

Example 2a demonstrates the steps for writing [latex]\mathrm{pass.^6_4}[/latex] to expand tonic, and Examples 2b and 2c show several ways [latex]\mathrm{pass.^6_4}[/latex] can prolong the predominant area. Note that each of these progressions can also work backward (e.g., [latex]\mathrm{I^6-pass.^6_4-I}[/latex] also works).

經過 pass[latex]^6_4[/latex] 的寫作步驟：

  1. 寫出完整低音。低音應為三個級進的音，第一個與最後一個音屬於相同的功能區域（T 或 PD）；中間的音就是經過音。
  2. 拼出 pass.[latex]\mathbf{^6_4}[/latex]。和 [latex]\mathrm{cad.^6_4}[/latex] 一樣，拼出 [latex]\mathrm{pass.^6_4}[/latex] 時，要找出低音上方四度與六度的音。另外要有一個聲部重複低音，也和 [latex]\mathrm{cad.^6_4}[/latex] 一樣。
  3. 寫出完整女高音。女高音應採用級進的旋律線，不要採用保持不動的聲部線。
  4. 填入內聲部，讓它們盡量少移動。

To write with pass[latex]^6_4[/latex]:

  1. Write the entire bass. You should have three notes in stepwise motion where the first and last notes belong to the same functional area (T or PD). The middle note will be your passing tone.
  2. Spell the pass.[latex]\mathbf{^6_4}[/latex]. Just like with [latex]\mathrm{cad.^6_4}[/latex], to spell [latex]\mathrm{pass.^6_4}[/latex], determine what notes are a fourth and sixth above the bass. One voice will double the bass, just like in [latex]\mathrm{cad.^6_4}[/latex].
  3. Write the entire soprano. The soprano should be a line that moves by step, not the static line.
  4. Fill in inner voices, making them move as little as possible.

Neighbor [latex]^6_4[/latex]

The neighbor (n.) [latex]^6_4[/latex] consists of a static bass over top of which two voices have upper-neighbor motion (Example 3). Sometimes [latex]\mathrm{n.^6_4}[/latex] is called pedal [latex]^6_4[/latex], a name that reflects the static pedal in the bass. It's most commonly found prolonging I or V. Example 4a demonstrates the steps for writing [latex]\mathrm{n.^6_4}[/latex] to prolong tonic, and Example 4b shows the voice leading to prolong V.

[latex]\mathrm{n.^6_4}[/latex] 的寫作步驟：

  1. 寫出完整低音。低音為三個相同的音，通常是 do–do–do 或 sol–sol–sol（[latex]\hat1-\hat1-\hat1[/latex] 或 [latex]\hat5-\hat5-\hat5[/latex]）。
  2. 拼出 n.[latex]\mathbf{^6_4}[/latex]。[latex]\mathrm{n.^6_4}[/latex] 配置在中間那個低音上。和 [latex]\mathrm{cad.}[/latex] 與 [latex]\mathrm{pass.^6_4}[/latex] 一樣，找出中間低音上方六度與四度的音，並以一個聲部重複低音。
  3. 寫出完整女高音。女高音可選擇作上鄰音進行，也可保持不動。與 [latex]\mathrm{cad.}[/latex] 或 [latex]\mathrm{pass.^6_4}[/latex] 相較，[latex]\mathrm{n.^6_4}[/latex] 更常採用保持不動的女高音。
  4. 填入內聲部，讓它們盡量少移動。

To write with [latex]\mathrm{n.^6_4}[/latex]:

  1. Write the entire bass. The bass will be three of the same note, typically do–do–do or sol–sol–sol ([latex]\hat1-\hat1-\hat1[/latex] or [latex]\hat5-\hat5-\hat5[/latex])
  2. Spell n.[latex]\mathbf{^6_4}[/latex]. The [latex]\mathrm{n.^6_4}[/latex] will be over the middle bass note. As with [latex]\mathrm{cad.}[/latex] and [latex]\mathrm{pass.^6_4}[/latex], determine a sixth and fourth above the middle bass note. One voice will double the bass.
  3. Write the entire soprano. For the soprano, choose either an upper-neighbor line or the static line. Unlike with [latex]\mathrm{cad.}[/latex] or [latex]\mathrm{pass.^6_4}[/latex], [latex]\mathrm{n.^6_4}[/latex] will more frequently have a static line in the soprano.
  4. Fill in the inner voices, making them move as little as possible.

琶音（arp.）[latex]^6_4[/latex] 通常由低音跳進到和弦五音、上方聲部繼續維持該和弦而形成。常見於結尾的低音琶音進行（例 3），或圓舞曲風格的伴奏（例 5）。與其他類型不同，[latex]\mathrm{arp.^6_4}[/latex] 在分析時通常不必另外標示。例 5 雖然用數字標出了它，但並非一定要這麼做；每個小節都可以直接標為 I。

Arpeggiating [latex]^6_4[/latex]

Arpeggiating (arp.) [latex]^6_4[/latex] is typically created when the bass leaps to the fifth of a chord while the upper voices sustain the chord. It's commonly found in, for example, ending bass arpeggiations (Example 3) or waltz-style accompaniments (Example 5). Unlike the other types, [latex]\mathrm{arp.^6_4}[/latex] typically doesn’t need to be labeled in analysis. Example 5 identifies it using figures, but it’s not necessary to do so—each measure could simply be labeled as I.

Summary: 6/4 chord types

The table in Example 6 summarizes the characteristics of the three [latex]^6_4[/latex] chord types that should be labeled in analysis. When you come across a [latex]^6_4[/latex] chord in analysis, remember to stop and ask yourself what type it is (passing, neighboring, or cadential) and label it appropriately.

6/4 chord Label Characteristics
Passing [latex]\mathrm{pass.^6_4}[/latex]
  • Passing tone in bass
  • Outer chords are same function``
Neighboring [latex]x^{5-6-5}_{3-4-3}[/latex]
  • Static bass
  • Upper neighbor motion in two upper voices
  • Outer chords are the same
Cadential [latex]\mathrm{V}\begin{smallmatrix}(8-7)\\6-5\\4-3\end{smallmatrix}[/latex]
  • Sol [latex](\hat{5})[/latex] in bass
  • [latex]^6_4[/latex] on stronger beat
