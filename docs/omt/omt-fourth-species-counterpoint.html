第四類對位法

Mark Gotham and Kris Shaffer

重點整理

類種對位法的第四類以掛留音為特色，並依下列步驟妥善處理：

  • 「協和預備」：掛留之前先構成協和音程。
  • 「不協和掛留」：一個聲部停留，另一個聲部移動，形成不協和音程。
  • 「協和解決」：先前停留的聲部開始移動，將不協和音程解決為協和音程。

在第四類對位法中，對位聲部與定旋律每小節各移動一次，但兩者在節奏上錯開一個二分音符的時值，可以想成小節層次的切分音。對位聲部以二分音符記譜，每個弱拍二分音符都用延音線跨越小節線，連到下一個強拍。這種安排使純粹的第四類對位法一律採用斜向進行，並引入一種新的不協和音：掛留音。

範例 1 收錄《Gradus ad Parnassum》第一部分的全部第四類對位法範例，並和先前一樣，標出對位聲部與定旋律之間的音程。《Gradus ad Parnassum》完整的練習、解答與註解，請見〈《Gradus ad Parnassum》練習〉。

Key Takeaways

The fourth species of species counterpoint is characterized by use of the suspension and its proper handling with:

  • "consonant preparation": a consonance interval before the suspension;
  • "dissonant suspension": one part stay while the other part moves to create a dissonant interval;
  • "consonant resolution": the part that stayed before now moves to resolve the dissonance to a consonance.

In fourth-species counterpoint, the counterpoint line and cantus firmus both move once per bar, but they are rhythmically offset from each other by a half note. (Think syncopation on the bar level.) The counterpoint line will be notated in half notes, with each weak-beat half note tied across the bar line to the following strong beat. This arrangement means that in pure fourth-species counterpoint, the two lines always move in oblique motion. It also introduces a new kind of dissonance: the suspension.

Example 1 provides the complete examples of fourth-species counterpoint from Part I of Gradus ad Parnassum, annotated (as before) with the interval that the counterpoint line makes with the cantus firmus. For the complete examples from Gradus ad Parnassum as exercises, solutions, and annotations, see Gradus ad Parnassum Exercises.

掛留音

掛留音是帶重音的不協和音，亦即它一律出現在強拍。由於受到更多強調，必須更細心地維持流暢與整體連貫。因此，與不協和經過音、鄰音一樣，掛留音的前後都由和聲上的協和音程包圍。掛留型態分為三個階段：

  • 預備：對位聲部的弱拍音，與定旋律協和。它用延音線連到掛留音，兩者音高相同。
  • 掛留：對位聲部的強拍音，從預備音延續而來，此時與定旋律不協和。
  • 解決：對位聲部的弱拍音，比預備與掛留的音高低一級，並與定旋律協和。

第四類對位法應盡可能使用不協和掛留，因為練習的主要目的正是學習如何處理它。

The Suspension

The suspension is an accented dissonance, meaning it always occurs on strong beats. Because of the increased emphasis, even greater care must be taken to promote smoothness and overall coherence. Thus, like the passing-tone and neighbor-tone dissonances, the suspension is always preceded and followed by harmonic consonances. A suspension figure has three parts:

  • Preparation: a weak-beat note in the counterpoint that is consonant with the cantus. This note is tied to the suspension, and the two are the same pitch.
  • Suspension: a strong-beat note in the counterpoint, tied from the preparation, that is dissonant with the cantus.
  • Resolution: a weak-beat note in the counterpoint that is one step below the preparation-suspension pitch and consonant with the cantus.

Use dissonant suspensions as much as possible in fourth-species counterpoint. The primary purpose of the exercise is to practice handling dissonant suspension.

掛留的類型

掛留依據掛留音與解決音在定旋律上方或下方所形成的音程分類（範例 2）。例如，7–6 掛留的強拍掛留音與定旋律構成七度，再向下級進，解決到弱拍與定旋律構成六度的音。

  • 定旋律上方可用的不協和掛留有 7–6、4–3 及 9–8（2–1）。只有這幾種型態能從不協和音程向下級進，解決到容許的協和音程。7–6 與 4–3 解決到不完全協和音程，因此較佳。
  • 定旋律下方主要使用的不協和掛留是 2–3。
  • 也可以使用 5–6 等其他進行，但保留的音並不造成不協和，因此不屬於不協和掛留。

Types of suspensions

Suspensions are categorized according to the intervals of the suspension and resolution tones above or below the cantus firmus (Example 2). A 7–6 suspension, for example, includes a strong-beat suspension that forms a seventh with the cantus, which resolves down by step to a weak-beat tone that forms a sixth with the cantus.

  • Possible dissonant suspensions above the cantus firmus are 7–6, 4–3, and 9–8 (2–1). These are the only options that start on a dissonance and resolve down by step to an allowable consonance. 7–6 and 4–3 are preferable due to the resolution to imperfect consonances.
  • The main dissonant suspension to use below the cantus firmus is 2–3.
  • Other motions like 5–6 can be used, but they do not constitute dissonant suspensions, as the suspended note is not dissonant.

如何使用掛留

第四類旋律建立的型態，會引導聽者把弱拍聽成主要的協和位置。因此，處理第四類掛留時，應依它解決後的音程，套用第一類的原則。可以多用定旋律上方的 7–6、4–3，或下方的 2–3、5–6，但同一型態最多連續三次，就像第一類中的三度與六度一樣。第一類不能連續使用兩個八度或兩個五度，所以也不要連續使用兩次 9–8、6–5 或 4–5 掛留。實際上，任何會在相鄰弱拍形成兩個五度或兩個八度的安排，都應避免；這稱為弱拍五度或八度（after-beat fifths or octaves）。

譯註：本段沿用原文較廣義的 suspension 說法，其中 5–6、6–5 的保留音不一定不協和。數字表示與當下定旋律的音程，不是對位聲部本身的旋律跳進度數；下方掛留向下解決時，與上方定旋律的距離反而增大。

Using suspensions

The pattern set forward by a fourth-species line invites listeners to interpret the weak beats as the main consonances, so treat suspensions in fourth species the same way you would treat their intervals of resolution in first species. Use 7–6 and 4–3 (above) or 2–3 and 5–6 (below) liberally, but no more than three times in a row (like thirds and sixths in first species). Since you cannot use two octaves or two fifths in a row in first species, do not use two 9–8, 6–5, or 4–5 suspensions in a row. In fact, avoid any configuration that would create two fifths or two octaves on consecutive weak beats in fourth species (after-beat fifths or octaves).

第四類對位聲部的旋律

只要可能，就使用不協和掛留。這會使旋律大多向下級進，也不容易朝向高潮發展，但沒有關係。只要旋律流暢、容易歌唱，而且掛留都有妥善預備與解決，就不必過度在意旋律形狀。第四類很難寫出與定旋律相同的輪廓品質，而它的主要目標是掛留處理，因此我們優先關注掛留，而非旋律形狀。

若無法使用不協和掛留，仍應嘗試用延音線從弱拍連到強拍。這可以形成「協和掛留」；你也可以從小節第一拍的協和音向上跳進到弱拍的協和音。至少需要一、兩次向上跳進，抵銷向下解決所造成的音域下移，讓旋律保持在容易歌唱的音域內。

如果不協和掛留與協和的延音型態都行不通，可以暫時中斷本類型態，見下方示範影片。這時改依第二類對位法的原則寫作，並儘快恢復第四類的延音線。每道練習盡量只中斷一次，而且只維持一、兩小節。

The Fourth-Species Counterpoint Line

Use dissonant suspensions whenever possible. This will create a line consisting mostly of downward stepwise motion, and it will also make it hard to direct motion toward a climax, but this is fine. Do not worry about the shape of the line as long as it is smooth and singable and the suspensions are properly prepared and resolved. (It is difficult to create a fourth-species counterpoint with the same shape as a cantus firmus, and the main goal of fourth species is the treatment of the suspensions, so we focus on that over melodic shape.)

If a dissonant suspension is not possible, try to use a tie from weak beat to strong beat. This can be a “consonant suspension,” or you can leap up from downbeat consonance to weak-beat consonance. At least one or two upward leaps will be necessary to counteract the downward resolutions in order to keep the line in a singable range.

If neither a dissonant suspension nor a consonant tied figure is possible, it is permissible to break species (see video demo below). When you break species, follow the principles of second-species counterpoint and resume fourth-species ties as soon as possible. Try not to break species more than once per exercise, and do so for just a bar or two.

Beginning a fourth-species counterpoint

Begin a fourth-species counterpoint above the cantus firmus with do [latex](\hat1)[/latex] or sol [latex](\hat5)[/latex]. Begin a fourth-species counterpoint below the cantus firmus with do [latex](\hat1)[/latex].

Always begin with a half rest.

第四類對位法的結束

第四類只有一種結尾方式：定旋律必須以 re–do [latex](\hat2-\hat1)[/latex] 結束。不要使用以 ti–do [latex](\hat7-\hat1)[/latex] 結束的定旋律。

對位聲部以不協和掛留導向結尾。倒數第二小節為 do–ti [latex](\hat1-\hat7)[/latex]，最後一小節則為全音符的 do [latex](\hat1)[/latex]。這個 do–ti [latex](\hat1-\hat7)[/latex] 若位於定旋律的 re [latex](\hat2)[/latex] 上方，便形成 7–6 掛留；若位於定旋律的 re [latex](\hat2)[/latex] 下方，則形成 2–3 掛留。作為不協和掛留的 do [latex](\hat1)[/latex]，一律由前一小節用延音線連過來。

查看英文原文

Ending a fourth-species counterpoint

There is only one option for ending fourth species. The cantus firmus must end with re–do [latex](\hat2-\hat1)[/latex]. Do not use a cantus that ends with ti–do [latex](\hat7-\hat1)[/latex].

The counterpoint will end with a dissonant suspension. The penultimate bar will contain do–ti [latex](\hat1-\hat7)[/latex], and the final bar will contain a whole note do [latex](\hat1)[/latex]. The do–ti [latex](\hat1-\hat7)[/latex] will form a 7–6 suspension above the re [latex](\hat2)[/latex] in the cantus, or a 2–3 suspension below the re [latex](\hat2)[/latex] in the cantus. As a dissonant suspension, that do [latex](\hat1)[/latex] will always be tied over from the previous bar.
