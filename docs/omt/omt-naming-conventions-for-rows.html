音列的命名慣例

Mark Gotham

重點整理

  • 本章討論十二音材料的不同表示方式：
    • 音高可以用音名或音高類表示。
    • 音列與變換可以採固定零點，讓P0從C開始；或採可移動零點，讓P0從分析者選定的音開始。
    • 矩陣也有配合這些命名慣例的不同排法。
  • 閱讀其他十二音分析文獻時，要準備面對各種慣例；自己分析時，選一種順手的方法，前後一致地使用即可。

分析十二音音樂時，音列、變換，甚至音高與音程的標記，都有不同慣例。本章比較分析文獻中最常遇到的主要方法。重點是音列與矩陣，但先從音高本身談起。

查看英文原文

Key Takeaways

  • This chapter goes through the different ways of representing twelve-tone material:
    • pitches by pitch name or pitch class;
    • rows and transformations with P0 starts on C (fixed zero) or P0 starts wherever we chose (moveable zero)
    • matrices, setting out these different row conventions.
  • When reading other writing on twelve-tone music, be prepared for any of these conventions to be used. But in your own work, simply choose one you feel comfortable with and use it consistently.

In analyzing twelve-tone music, there are different conventions for labeling rows, transformations, and even pitches and intervals. This chapter compares the main approaches that you’re most likely to encounter in analytical writings. The focus is on rows and matrices, but before we get to that, let’s deal first with the pitches themselves.

音高

如前面章節所述，有些分析適合用音高類記法，也就是從C＝0到B＝11的整數，取代C♯或D♭等具體拼寫。這種慣例多用於非調性音樂，包括大部分十二音音樂；它方便進行音高類集合分析與十二音音樂中的數學運算，也暫時避開音名拼寫問題。十二音作品的拼寫往往仍有其邏輯，只是邏輯可能不同，也較難概括。例如，用特定音名寫出音列時，通常不像調性音階的音名那樣，反映音高階序或調性。

查看英文原文

Pitch

As we’ve seen earlier in the book, it is useful in some analytical contexts to use pitch-class notation (integers from 0 for C to 11 for B) as an alternative to spelling out those pitches (e.g., C♯ vs D♭). This convention is mostly associated with non-tonal music (including most twelve-tone music), where it can be handy for performing the kinds of mathematical operations we’ve seen (in both pitch-class set analysis and twelve-tone music) and for sidestepping questions of pitch spelling. There’s often still a logic to the pitch spellings used in a twelve-tone piece, but that logic is often of a different and perhaps less generalizable kind. For instance, using specific pitch spellings in a row-form representation usually doesn’t reflect a hierarchy or tonality in the same way that the pitches of a scale do in tonal music.

音列

音列記法與標號的主要差別，在於以哪個音作為組織的參照點：

  1. 不論分析什麼作品，都採用相同的音，慣例上是C。
  2. 選一個對當前音樂脈絡具有重要性的音。

例如，〈十二音理論基礎〉把Elisabeth Lutyens《經文歌》的音列從C寫起，得到0–11–3–7–8–4–2–6–5–1–9–10。另一種做法是讓P0從D開始，因為最先進入的女低音從D4起唱，並在這個音高位置唱出原形音列的前六音組。[1]如此一來，P0便是2–1–5–9–10–6–4–8–7–3–11–0。

譯註：這裡的0到11仍是固定音高類整數，C＝0、D＝2；改變的是P₀這個音列形式名稱所指的位置。第二個音列的每個數字，都是第一個加2後取模12。
查看英文原文

Rows

For rows, the main difference in notation and labeling centers on a single choice about which pitch to organize our rows around:

  1. the same pitch in all contexts (conventionally, that pitch is C)
  2. a pitch that’s important to the musical context in question

For instance, in the Basics of Twelve-Tone Theory chapter, we set out the row of Elisabeth Lutyens’s Motet starting on C, which gave us the twelve-tone row 0–11–3–7–8–4–2–6–5–1–9–10. Alternatively, we could set out the P0 starting on D, as the first voice to enter (alto) starts on D4 and proceeds to sing the first hexachord of this prime-form row on that pitch level.[1] That would give us a P0 of 2–1–5–9–10–6–4–8–7–3–11–0.

選項一：P0從C開始——固定零點

在這種慣例中，不論你選哪個方向的音列作為原形，將它移位到C起音的形式就是P0。原文認為這可能是最常見的慣例；它有時稱為「以零為中心」或「固定零點」，這是借用調性音樂唱名系統的類比。

既然P0從C開始，I0也從C開始，而R0與RI0則在C結束。這樣區分，是為了讓P0與R0保持精確的逆行關係，I0與RI0也一樣。理論上，當然可以設計另一種更一致地「以零為中心」的系統，讓P0、I0、R0、RI0都從C開始，但這並不是廣泛採用的慣例。

整理如下：

  • P0從C開始。
  • I0從C開始，與P0的起始音高類相同。
  • R0從P0的最後一音開始；完整十二音音列每個音高類只出現一次，因此這一音不會是C。
  • RI0從I0的最後一音開始，同樣不會是C。
譯註：「最後一音不是C」以本章標準十二音音列為前提：十二個不同音高類各出現一次。這不能直接套到上一章Tavener那種含重複音、也不是十二音的短序列。
查看英文原文

Option 1: P0 starts on C (fixed zero)

In this convention, whatever you decide the prime form to be, the transposition of that form starting on C is P0. This is probably the most common convention today, and sometimes called “zero-centered” or “fixed-zero” (by analogy to tonal solfège systems).

As we have set P0 to begin on C, I0 also begins on C, and R0 and RI0 will end on C. This separation of P0 and I0 from R0 and RI0 makes sense because we prefer P0 and R0 to be exact retrogrades of one other (and likewise I0 and RI0). We could theoretically have an even more consistently “zero-centered” system in which all of P0, I0, R0 and RI0 begin on C, but that’s not a convention that people have widely adopted.

In summary:

  • P0 starts with C
  • I0 starts with C (same pitch class as P0)
  • R0 starts with the last note of P0 (by definition, not C)
  • RI0 starts with the last note of I0 (by definition, not C)

選項二：P0從選定的音開始——可移動零點

另一種方法，是把作品最先出現，或最有意義的音列形式指定為P0，不論它從哪個音高類開始。這個選擇可能在作品中很明顯，可能從分析推導出來，也可能帶有一定的任意性。之後的移位與其他運算，仍以相同方式進行，只是改以這個P0為參照。延續唱名系統的類比，這種慣例有時稱為「以原始音列為中心」或「可移動零點」。

譯註：可移動零點並沒有把所有音高類數字重新定義成相對音級。在本章矩陣第二種中，D仍寫2，但D起音的原形稱P₀；從這個參照再升高一個半音的形式稱P₁。其I₀也從D開始，若用音高類集合論的固定倒影運算表示，需用I₄(x)=4−x（mod12），而不是I₀(x)=−x。

整理如下：

  • P0的位置由分析者選定，因此起始音也由分析者決定。
  • I0仍與P0從同一個音開始。
  • R0仍從P0的最後一音開始。
  • RI0仍從I0的最後一音開始。

Option 2: P0 starts wherever we chose (moveable zero)

In the alternative method, the P0 form is assigned to either the first form of the row or the one that is most meaningful—regardless of what pitch class begins the row. Depending on the context, this may be evident from the piece, deduced from the analysis, or allocated semi-arbitrarily. Transpositions and other operations are then worked out in the same way, in relation to that P0 form. This convention is sometimes called “original-centered” or “movable-zero” (to continue the solfège analogy).

In summary:

  • P0 takes a transposition (and thus starts with a pitch) chosen by the analyst
  • I0 still starts with the same note as P0
  • R0 still starts with the last note of P0
  • RI0 still starts with the last note of I0

兩種方法的差異與取捨

從上面兩份整理可以看出，兩種命名慣例其實沒有那麼不同。無論採哪套命名，移位與其他運算本身的方式都一樣；主要差別是起點，也就是選哪個音列形式，作為聯繫其他形式的參照。

譯註：命名慣例不改變移位、倒影或逆行如何作用於音高類。改變的是參照音列及形式下標的解讀；不同方法的同一個P₀或I₀名稱，未必代表相同的實際音高排列。

如同許多並行的命名慣例，兩者各有優缺點。若作品的音樂脈絡促使你特別指定P0，可移動零點可能比較合用；但採這個方法後，你可能覺得每次分析都必須替P0的位置找一個「好理由」，而這不一定總是適當。固定零點的優點是清楚、一致；原文認為，這可能使它在近年的研究中更常見，但不表示它必然「比較好」。

事實上，很多時候，連哪個方向應稱為P、哪個應稱為I或R，都不見得清楚。這類判斷目前也沒有一套廣泛公認的決定方法。

Same? Different? Better? Worse?

As the two summaries suggest, these naming conventions are actually not so different. It bears repeating that for all naming systems, transposition and the other operations all work in the same way, so it’s mostly just a matter of where you start: which row form you use as the referential form to relate others to.

And as is so often the case when multiple parallel naming conventions emerge, there are both benefits and downsides to each approach. If you’re analyzing music that makes you want to assign P0 in a musically sensitive way, then the moveable-zero convention may suit your purposes. But if you go down that route, then you’ll probably feel compelled to come up with a “good” reason for the pitch level of P0 in all your analyses, and that may not always be appropriate. The fixed-zero system has the benefit of clarity and consistency. That’s probably why it’s become more common in recent scholarship, but that doesn’t necessarily make it “better.”

Indeed, in many cases, it won’t even be clear which orientation should be P and which I (or R for that matter). Unfortunately, there isn’t yet a widely recognized system for making such determinations.

第三種：固定零點編號，從D開始的P2置頂

或許最容易令人困惑的是一種混合排法：仍把D起音的版本放在最上方，但這次稱它為P2。也就是：

  • 以選定的音高或移位位置來安排矩陣，本例選D。
  • 音列名稱仍採另一個選項：固定零點，讓P0表示C起音的原形。

Type 3

Perhaps most confusing of all is a kind of hybrid version where we still have the D version on the top row, but now we label it P2. So:

  • We organize the row class around a chosen pitch/transposition (here D).
  • We still label the row forms around the alternative option (P0 starts on C).

Note how this time, comparing it with the version above, the pitches have stayed the same, but the lists of row forms have changed (Px, Py…).

整理

因此，最上方橫列可能是：

  • 從音高類0開始的P0。
  • 從音高類n開始的P0，本例n＝2。
  • 從音高類n開始的Pn。

這些命名與矩陣生成慣例都有人使用。知道各種選項後，遇到矩陣時先確認採用哪一套，尤其是沒有明確標示音列名稱的矩陣。

Summary

In summary, the first row can read:

  • P0, starting on 0
  • P0, starting on n (here 2)
  • Pn, starting on n

All of these naming and matrix-generating conventions are out there. It’s best simply to be aware of these options and check that you have the right convention in mind when you come across one (especially where the matrices neglect to explicitly label the row names).
