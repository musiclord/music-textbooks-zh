藍調旋律與藍調音階

Megan Lavengood

重點整理

  • 有歌詞的藍調，常先唱一句、再重複一次，最後接對比句，形成aab；旋律也經常採用相同結構。
  • 藍調旋律常留下較長空隙，讓主奏聲部與其他樂器呼應。
  • 藍調音階相當於小調五聲音階，再加一個半音經過音：do–me–fa–fi–sol–te[latex](\hat1-\downarrow\hat3-\hat4-\uparrow\hat4-\hat5-\downarrow\hat7)[/latex]。
  • 把藍調音階旋轉，改從第二個音開始，就能得到大調藍調音階：do–re–ri–mi–sol–la[latex](\hat1-\hat2-\uparrow\hat2-\hat3-\hat5-\hat6)[/latex]。
譯註：旋轉後的主音會改變；不是在同一主音上只換一個開始位置。例如A小調藍調音階A–C–D–D♯–E–G，從C開始可等音記為C–D–E♭–E–G–A，即C大調藍調音階；原文用ri時，E♭可等音寫成D♯。

本章探討幾種塑造今日藍調的旋律特徵，並以Clarence Williams的〈Gulf Coast Blues〉為主要例子。它屬於最早錄製成唱片的一批藍調歌曲，由商業成就卓著的歌手Bessie Smith於1923年錄製。

Key Takeaways

  • In a blues song with a sung text, the lyrics consist of a line that is repeated, then followed by a contrasting line (aab). The melody often follows this structure as well.
  • Blues melodies often leave large gaps to allow for call-and-response between the melodic instrument and other instruments.
  • The blues scale is like a minor pentatonic scale with an additional chromatic passing tone: do–me–fa–fi–sol–te [latex](\hat1-\downarrow\hat3-\hat4-\uparrow\hat4-\hat5-\downarrow\hat7)[/latex].
  • The blues scale can be rotated to begin on its second note, creating a major blues scale: do–re–ri–mi–sol–la [latex](\hat1-\hat2-\uparrow\hat2-\hat3-\hat5-\hat6)[/latex].

This chapter discusses some of the trends in blues melodies that shaped the blues as we know it today. As an example, this text will focus on one of the earliest recorded blues songs, "Gulf Coast Blues" by Clarence Williams, as recorded by the enormously commercially successful blues singer Bessie Smith in 1923.

Spotify Embed: Gulf Coast Blues

Example 1. "Gulf Coast Blues" (1923), recording by Bessie Smith and Clarence Williams.

樂句與歌詞結構

許多藍調作品有人聲演唱，因此歌詞很重要。十二小節藍調的三個四小節樂句，常配合aab歌詞：第一句唱出後再重複一次，有時稍作變化，第三句則形成對比。Clarence Williams的〈Gulf Coast Blues〉（1923）就是一例（例2）。重複的歌詞也常配上重複旋律，呼應aab結構；不過，〈Gulf Coast Blues〉並沒有這樣處理旋律。

結構歌詞
a我愛的那個男人，已經離開了這座城。
a我愛的那個男人，已經離開了這座城。
b要是雨還一直下，我就往墨西哥灣沿岸去。

例2。Clarence Williams〈Gulf Coast Blues〉的歌詞。

藍調樂句另一個重要特徵，是呼應（call-and-response）；它可能承襲了遭受奴役的非洲人與非裔美國人的勞動歌曲。帶歌詞的人聲旋律負責「呼」，樂器填入的樂句則負責「應」。注意，在〈Gulf Coast Blues〉中，每句標為a的歌詞，都只在該樂句前兩小節唱完。例3在採譜上標出這種呼應關係。

譯註：歌詞位置與呼應分析依原章採譜說明保留；原錄音入口可外連，本輪未播放錄音或獨立驗證歌手的節拍位置。

Phrase and Lyric Structure

Much blues music is sung, and so lyrics play an important role in this genre. The four-bar phrases that make up the 12-bar blues are commonly matched with lyrics that have an aab structure: the first line is stated and then repeated (sometimes with some alteration), and the third line contrasts. "Gulf Coast Blues" by Clarence Williams (1923) is one example of this (Example 2). The repeated lyric will often be set to a repeated melody, mimicking the aab structure of the lyrics, though this does not happen in “Gulf Coast Blues.”

structure lyric
a The man I love, he has done left this town
a The man I love, he has done left this town
b And if it keeps on raining, I will be Gulf Coast bound.

Example 2. Lyrics of "Gulf Coast Blues" by Clarence Williams.

Another essential part of blues phrase structure is the notion of call-and-response, a feature likely inherited from the work songs of enslaved Africans and African Americans. The vocal, lyricized melody takes on the role of the "call" while an instrumental filler takes on the role of the "response." Notice that in "Gulf Coast Blues," each lyric labeled with an a is sung entirely and exclusively in the first two measures of the phrase. Example 3 annotates a transcription of "Gulf Coast Blues" to show this call-and-response relationship.

藍調音階

如同藍調和聲往往不侷限於同一套自然音材料，旋律也會使用相應的半音語彙。例4上方譜表的藍調音階，試著把藍調旋律慣例整理成一種音階，供初學者作為即興基礎。它基本上是小調五聲音階，再加一個向上導往sol[latex](\hat5)[/latex]的半音經過音。

音樂譜例

靜態樂譜待補；原始資源包含音樂譜例。

例4。C藍調音階與C大調的I、V和弦之間，產生具有風格特色的音高碰撞。

即使與下方和聲有音高衝突，這套藍調音階仍可用於大調與小調藍調。把它和大調藍調的I、IV、V，也就是C調中的C大三和弦、F大三和弦、G大三和弦結合時，mi/me[latex](\hat3/\downarrow\hat3)[/latex]與ti/te[latex](\hat7/\downarrow\hat7)[/latex]之間的碰撞，尤其明顯。

譯註：原文列I、V、IV，卻依序寫C、F、G，次序不一致；中文更正羅馬數字為I、IV、V，與和弦音名對應。

這些音高的交錯，也常呈現為藍調音：實際音高既不是完整的降音，也不是通常的自然音，而落在兩者之間，彷彿介於mi/me[latex](\hat3/\downarrow\hat3)[/latex]，或ti/te[latex](\hat7/\downarrow\hat7)[/latex]之間。

譯註：這裡描述演唱、滑音或彎音等實際音高處理，不是說同時彈兩個十二平均律音，就會自動產生正中間的新音。藍調音也不必固定在四分音或恰好中點，實際音高可隨語境與表情改變。

The Blues Scale

Much as the harmonies of the blues tend not to stick to one diatonic key, flouting the norms of tonal music, the melodies are similarly chromatic to match. The blues scale, notated in the upper staff of Example 4, attempts to generalize blues melodic practice into a scale on which beginning improvisers can base their melodies. The blues scale is essentially a minor pentatonic scale with an added chromatic passing tone leading up to sol [latex](\hat5)[/latex].

Musical example

Static score pending; the original resource contains a musical example.

Example 4. The C blues scale creates stylistic clashes with the I and V chords of C major.

This blues scale is used in both major and minor blues tunes, despite the clashes with the underlying harmony. When this scale is combined with the chords of the major blues—I, V, and IV, or C major, F major, and G major in the key of C—the characteristic clashes between mi/me [latex](\hat3/\downarrow\hat3)[/latex] and ti/te [latex](\hat7/\downarrow\hat7)[/latex] are especially notable.

These clashes often produce blue notes—notes that are not really flat or natural, but somewhere in between. Blue notes seem to split the difference between mi/me [latex](\hat3/\downarrow\hat3)[/latex] or ti/te [latex](\hat7/\downarrow\hat7)[/latex].

「大調」藍調音階

有些樂手覺得，把大調藍調音階獨立出來思考很有幫助。大調與小調五聲音階的關係，也適用於大、小調藍調音階：大調藍調音階，可以由關係小調的藍調音階旋轉得到。從原音階的me[latex](\downarrow\hat3)[/latex]開始，就得到關係大調的藍調音階。例5整理了這些關係。

音樂譜例

靜態樂譜待補；原始資源包含音樂譜例。

例5。把藍調音階旋轉，從第二個音開始，就得到大調藍調音階。

相較於小調藍調音階，大調藍調音階與大三和弦之間的不協和較少。即興時，可以試著在藍調進行的大和弦上使用大調藍調音階。不過，別忘了，在大和弦上使用帶有降三、降七級的藍調音階，也完全符合藍調慣例。

The "major" blues scale

Some improvisers find it helpful to think of a major blues scale. The difference between a major and minor pentatonic scale is identical to the difference between the major and minor blues scale: the major blues scale is a rotation of the blues scale of its relative minor. Begin the blues scale on me [latex](\downarrow\hat3)[/latex], and you will get a blues scale for the relative major. These relationships are summarized in Example 5.

Musical example

Static score pending; the original resource contains a musical example.

Example 5. Rotating the blues scale to begin on its second note yields the major blues scale.

Compared to the minor blues scale, the major blues scale is less dissonant with major chords. When improvising, it can be helpful to think of improvising with the major blues scale over the major chords of the blues progression. But remember that using the blues scale (with flatted thirds and sevenths) over major chords is also a perfectly normal practice.
